# Hood Cleaning Location Page Template

Common page outline for hood cleaning clients (e.g., Advanced Hood Cleaning Solutions). It mirrors the tree service location page, modified for commercial kitchen hood cleaning. Replace [City, ST] and [Business Name] for each page.

Apply `tone-and-qa-rules.md` for tone, sentence length, banned words, and punctuation. The tree-only parts of that file (Service Definitions, tree Required Terms like "arborist" and "tree surgeons") do not apply.

The person usually supplies local information with the request (local kitchen types, neighborhoods, codes). Work it in naturally throughout the page.

## Page Structure

### Meta Description
24 words. Start with "Get."

### H1: Hood Cleaning in [City, ST] [40 words]
First talk about the conditions that happen in commercial hoods over time. Then introduce our service as the solution, and mention our features or USPs.

### H2: Reliable Local Kitchen Hood Cleaners at Your Service [100 words]
Localize the content. Mention local commercial kitchen types, what they usually cook, the problems in their hoods, and how our team solves them. Also mention specific regulations, and that we know them and comply with them.

### H2: Our Hood Cleaning Services
- First, 30 words saying we offer full hood cleaning solutions (do not use the banned word "comprehensive").
- Then 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences for each service below. Say exactly what we do and the benefits.
- H3s (use the client's service list):
  - Duct Repairs
  - Pressure Washing
  - Duct Installation
  - Fire Suppression Service

### H2: Why Choose [Business Name]
- First, 20 to 22 words saying the community trusts us.
- Then 4 USPs that will convince customers to hire us, related to hood cleaning.
- Each USP is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do.

### H2: Our Working Process
- First, mention the business name and say we follow a safe method.
- Then 4 steps related to hood cleaning.
- Each step is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do in that step.

### H2: We Bring Modern Hood Cleaning Service Throughout [City]
- First, 20 words saying we serve every side of the town.
- Then 10 to 12 words for each local area (from the local information provided). Talk about the commercial kitchens around each area.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Precise answer to each question in 22 to 25 words. Give the direct answer first, then promote our service.

- How often should a hood system be cleaned in [City]?
- How long does it take to clean a kitchen hood?
- How much does hood cleaning in [City] cost?
