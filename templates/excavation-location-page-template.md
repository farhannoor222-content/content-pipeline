# Excavation Location Page Template

Common page outline for excavation clients (e.g., Bonzo Excavating). Same structure as the hood cleaning location page (`hood-cleaning-location-page-template.md`), modified for excavation. Replace [City, ST] and [Business Name] for each page.

Apply `tone-and-qa-rules.md` for tone, sentence length, banned words, and punctuation. The tree-only parts of that file (Service Definitions, tree Required Terms like "arborist" and "tree surgeons") do not apply.

The person usually supplies local information with the request (local building or property types, neighborhoods, codes). Work it in naturally throughout the page.

## Page Structure

### Meta Description
24 words. Start with "Get."

### H1: Excavation in [City, ST] [40 words]
First talk about the ground conditions that cause problems on properties over time (poor drainage, soil movement, erosion, uneven grading). Then introduce our service as the solution, and mention our features or USPs.

### H2: Reliable Local Excavation Contractors at Your Service [100 words]
Localize the content. Mention local property types, soil and terrain, drainage patterns, the problems property owners face, and how our team solves them. Also mention specific rules (for example, calling 811 before digging, county septic and grading permits, erosion and sediment control), and that we know them and comply with them.

### H2: Our Excavation Services
- First, 30 words saying we offer full excavation solutions (do not use the banned word "comprehensive").
- Then 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences for each service. Say exactly what we do and the benefits.
- H3s: use the client's service list, for example:
  - Septic Installation
  - Utility Installation

### H2: Why Choose [Business Name]
- First, 20 to 22 words saying the community trusts us.
- Then 4 USPs that will convince customers to hire us, related to excavation.
- Each USP is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do.

### H2: Our Working Process
- First, mention the business name and say we follow a safe method.
- Then 4 steps related to excavation.
- Each step is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do in that step.

### H2: We Bring Modern Excavation Service Throughout [City]
- First, 20 words saying we serve every side of the town.
- Then 10 to 12 words for each local area (from the local information provided). Talk about the properties, terrain, and soil around each area.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Precise answer to each question in 22 to 25 words. Give the direct answer first, then promote our service.

- How long does an excavation project take?
- Do I need a permit for excavation in [City]?
- How much does excavation in [City] cost?

Note: the FAQ questions and H2 wording above were adapted from the hood cleaning outline. Adjust them if the person gives different ones.
