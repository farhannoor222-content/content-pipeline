# Excavation Entity Page Template

Use this template for an "excavation service near [Location Name]" page. The person gives a location name and some details about the place. The place is a small landmark or area inside a city (a neighborhood, school, church, station, stadium, business district, industrial district, subdivision, commercial area, or community landmark).

Focus on the landmark given, not the city. The city stays secondary throughout the content.

Apply the client's files (`about.md`, `customer-persona.md`, `pipeline-notes.md`) for business details, services, and base address.

## Final Structure Summary

- Meta Description (no heading)
- H1: Location overview and excavation-related discussion
- H2: Professional excavating service from our company
- H2: Driving directions from our base

## Part 1: Meta Description

Write a meta description of 155 characters. Bold the term "meta description:" at the start.

- Start with an action verb, like get, find, etc.
- Must include the term "excavation service near"
- Must mention and bold "excavation service near" + location name
- Don't mention other service names
- No phone number
- Mention the benefits of our services instead

Example of a good meta description:

> Get expert excavation service near... We provide site preparation, modern equipment, and fair pricing for residential and commercial projects. Call today.

## Part 2: H1 Section

H1: "Excavation Service Near [Location Name]," given a strong title that includes the location name and something meaningful about the area.

- 100 words maximum, in 2 to 3 paragraphs. Don't write more than 100 words.
- In the first 1 to 2 paragraphs, write only about this location. First explain where it is located, then discuss its impact. Use the information provided.
- Don't mention the business name in this section. Focus on the specific place itself. If it's a church, neighborhood, stadium, school, industrial district, commercial area, or community landmark, discuss that first.
- Talk about:
  - Its history
  - Its role in the community
  - What the area is known for
  - What it is like around nearby properties and neighborhoods
  - Common land conditions, drainage concerns, grading issues, soil movement, erosion, flooding, or construction challenges in the area
- Then transition naturally into excavation topics: common land preparation issues, drainage concerns, uneven terrain, soil stability, trenching needs, or grading challenges nearby properties face. The shift from location discussion to excavation topics should feel natural.
- Gather information from SERP, Reddit, and Facebook as well.
- No em dashes (locked rule).
- Aim for 8th-grade readability.

## Writing Instructions (all sections)

### 1. Voice
Confident, professional, and reassuring, like an experienced local contractor. Avoid exaggeration or hype. Focus on reliability, preparation, workmanship, safety, and proper cleanup.

### 2. Reading Level
8th-grade maximum (Hemingway).

- Start sentences mostly with subjects instead of phrases
- Short sentences of 10 to 15 words
- Active voice
- Present tense

### 3. Perspective
Use second person ("you," "your property") to keep the content relatable. Start some sentences with "You" and write like a strong local copywriter.

### 4. Emotional Anchors
Safety, trust, workmanship, drainage protection, property stability, and clean project completion.

### 5. Constraints and Precision Rules

- Avoid fluff phrases like "we are passionate" or "we care deeply"
- Avoid adverbs whenever possible
- Avoid words like very, super, actually, etc.
- Use strong verbs and compound adjectives when suitable
- Avoid the terms "anchor" and "sits"

Examples:

- Bad: We simultaneously left the building at the same time.
- Good: We left the building at the same time.
- Bad: The contractor explains the project in a very clear and concise manner.
- Good: The contractor explains the project clearly and answers questions right away.
- Bad: Our Excavating Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care, precision, and complete debris removal, leaving your property clean and ready for use.
- Good: Our Excavating Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care, precision, and complete debris removal. The property stays clean and ready for use.

## Part 3: H2 Section About Our Excavation Service

Add an H2 about how the area has access to dependable excavation service. Modify the heading for better user experience.

- 100 words
- Aim for 8th-grade readability
- Promote the business in the location mentioned in the headline
- Mention the brand name in the first line
- Explain how we are a local team
- Mention our excavation services and benefits that encourage readers to hire us. Use information from the client's project files when needed.
- Mention nearby neighborhoods and surrounding communities naturally
- Discuss:
  - Site preparation
  - Land grading
  - Drainage solutions
  - Trenching
  - Foundation excavation
  - Land clearing
  - Soil leveling
  - Equipment used for excavation projects
- Mention how our work benefits nearby properties and construction projects
- Explain that our team follows a customized approach based on the area's terrain, drainage patterns, soil conditions, and weather
- Avoid:
  - Em dashes
  - Comma + -ing verbs
  - Starting sentences with "You get"
  - Starting sentences with "We handle"
- The tone stays professional, local, and trustworthy.

## Part 4: Driving Directions Section

Add an H2 that shows the route we take from our base to the destination. Mention the company name in the H2.

The person pastes driving directions from Google Maps. Convert them into conversational instructions.

- Before the bullet points, add a short conversational intro. Example: "ABC Excavating is only 6 miles away, which takes about 15 minutes for arrival."
- 4 to 6 bullet points
- Each bullet contains 10 to 15 words
- Avoid comma + -ing verbs
- Avoid em dashes
- The directions should sound like our crew travels this route to reach the destination. They must not sound like customer instructions.
- "We," "our," and "us" are allowed, but don't start every sentence with them.
- Keep the bullets easy to follow and natural, similar to Google Maps directions.
- After the bullet points, end with a short sentence explaining that if traffic or road closures affect this route, alternate roads are available because we know the area well.

## Important

Focus on the exact place given, whether it is a neighborhood, school, church, station, business district, subdivision, or local landmark. The city should remain secondary throughout the content.
