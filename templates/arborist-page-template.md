# Arborist Page Template

Use this template for an "Arborist Page": a page that promotes the client's arborist service in one city. Usually one page per business, sometimes 2 to 3 pages for different locations (one page per location, each fully localized and varied from the others).

Apply all rules in `tone-and-qa-rules.md` (voice, reading level, perspective, formatting, global banned words, Required Terms, Service Definitions, QA checklist). Where this template and the global rules seem to conflict, the notes in "Wording Rules for This Template" below settle it.

## Research

- **Localization (always):** city population and character, local tree species, climate, soil, common tree risks, and real neighborhoods or areas (both tight older lots and wider newer or rural lots). Use only verified facts.
- **Competitor analysis (when available):** search "arborist [City, ST]" and "arborist service [City, ST]". Arborist pages are a low-competition niche, so often no useful competitor page exists. That is fine. Write from localization and the client profile, and note "no usable competitors" in the tracker.
- **Skip listing sites entirely:** Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, BBB, Yellow Pages, Nextdoor, Houzz, Porch, Expertise.com, ThreeBest Rated, Bark, Manta, MapQuest, and any "top 10 arborists in [City]" roundup or directory. These are not competitors and are not research sources.
- Apply the Thin-Content Filter and the three tests (specific, adds something new, not boilerplate) from `tone-and-qa-rules.md` to anything taken from a real competitor page.

## Wording Rules for This Template

- **Never use:** "study," "training," "train," "trained" (or any form of them). Also never use the global banned words. The ones most likely to slip in on this page are "skilled," "ensure," "best," "top," "peace of mind," and "enhance."
  - Where the template asks for "trained professionals" or "skilled experts," write "experienced," "qualified," "knowledgeable," or "hands-on" instead.
- **Claims:** "arborist" and "our arborists" are fine for every client. Never add "certified" or "ISA-certified" unless `about.md` mentions a certification. Use "licensed," "insured," "affordable," "free quotes," or "flat pricing" only when the client profile supports them. If it doesn't, swap in a trait it does confirm (e.g., "experienced," "local," "free estimates").
- **Equipment:** say generally that heavy equipment is used only when a job needs it, with smaller tools otherwise to protect the yard. Don't name specific machines unless the client profile names them.
- Tree Removal (section by section) and Tree Cutting (felling in one piece) must stay clearly distinct, per Service Definitions.

## Page Structure

### Meta Description
Base: "Get reliable arborist service in [City], [ST], from licensed, insured, and experienced professionals at affordable pricing with free upfront quotes. Call now."
Keep about the same length and must include "arborist service in [City], [ST]." Swap any unconfirmed claim (see Claims rule). Vary the wording slightly between pages for the same client.

### H1: Arborist Service in [City], [ST]
Body: 40 words.
- Open by describing how DIY tree care affects tree health (e.g., bad cuts, topping, wounds that invite decay or pests, weakened limbs). Rotate the DIY angle; see Variation Rules.
- Then introduce the business as the problem solver and the benefits it offers.
- Must include the exact phrase "arborist service in [City], [ST]."

### H2: Why Choose Us for Arborist Care?
Intro: 30 words. Say the company brings experienced arborists, and name the benefits.

Then 4 H3s:
- Pick the 4 most relevant, customer-friendly USPs from the client's `about.md`, `customer-persona.md`, and `pipeline-notes.md` USP list. They must relate to arborist work (tree health, proper pruning cuts, diagnosis, property protection, local tree knowledge, clear quotes, cleanup, response time), not generic filler.
- H3 headings: 3 to 4 words.
- Each H3 body: 20 to 25 words, 2 sentences, explaining the benefit the customer gets.

### H2: Our Services for Homes and Businesses
Intro: 20 words. Say our arborists are experienced, qualified professionals who can address any tree problem. (Never "trained.")

Then one H3 per service, each 20 to 22 words, 2 sentences, explaining what our arborists do in that service:
- **Tree Removal**: never use the word "dead." Describe section-by-section removal.
- **Tree Trimming**: never use the word "regular."
- **Stump Grinding**
- **Emergency Tree Service**
- **Tree Cutting Service**: felling in one piece.

Linking: link each H3 to the client's own matching service page URL. Use the URLs the person gives in the request; otherwise use the service URLs in the client's `pipeline-notes.md` or `about.md`. If none exist, leave the H3s unlinked and say so in the final message. (The original template linked to wallawallatreeservice.com. That was one client's example, so never reuse those URLs for another client.)

If the client doesn't offer one of these services, drop it and substitute the closest service the client does offer from its services list. Never list a service the client doesn't offer.

### H2: Avoid DIY Risks with Our Experienced Professionals
Body: 100 to 120 words. Cover:
- The risks of DIY tree care (falls, saw injuries, limbs landing on roofs or power lines, bad cuts that harm the tree).
- How our experienced arborists avoid those risks and give your trees and property proper care. (Don't use "ensure.")
- That good tree care also raises property value, plus one or two other benefits.
- Localization: name 2 to 3 local tree species, the local climate, and both tight and wider neighborhoods where the crew regularly works (real neighborhood names from research).
- Heavy equipment is used only when it's necessary. Otherwise the crew prefers smaller tools to avoid yard damage.

### H2: Schedule a Free Consultation Today
Body: 40 words. Ask the reader to contact the business for a consultation, explain what the consultation includes (e.g., on-site look at the trees, health and risk check, recommended work, upfront quote) and its benefits.
- No phone number.
- Use the brand name exactly once.

### H2: Our Arborist Service Is Available Throughout [City]
Intro: 20 words, saying the business serves the whole town.

Then a bulleted list of areas (3 by default, or the ones the person gives):
- Bullet format: **Area Name**: then a 10 to 12 word description introducing what that area is (character, setting, housing, trees), without repeating the area's name in the description.
- Pick real neighborhoods, districts, or nearby communities from research. Mix tight older areas and wider lot areas where possible.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions
3 questions, each answer 22 to 25 words. First sentence gives a direct answer. Second sentence promotes the business's quality solutions using "our" (e.g., "Our arborists..."). Never use the business name in FAQ answers.

1. Why should I hire an arborist instead of a general tree lopper?
2. How much do arborists charge per hour in [City]?
   - Answer: about $200 to $400 per hour, but our team gives an upfront flat price with a free quote. If local research shows a clearly different range, or the client profile says it bills hourly, adjust and flag it in the final message.
3. When is the best time to call an arborist in [City]?
   - Tie the answer to the local climate and season (e.g., dormant season for pruning, right after storms for damage).

## Variation Rules

H2 headings may be lightly reworded per page so pages don't look templated. Keep the meaning and keep the key term ("arborist," "DIY," "free consultation," the city name) where the base heading has it. Check the client's Arborist Page Tracker and don't reuse a variant used on that client's last 2 to 3 arborist pages. On multi-location requests for the same client, every page must use a different variant for each rewordable H2.

| Base H2 | Example variants |
|---|---|
| Why Choose Us for Arborist Care? | Why Homeowners Pick Us for Arborist Care / What Sets Our Arborist Care Apart / Reasons to Choose Our Arborists |
| Our Services for Homes and Businesses | Arborist Services for Homes and Businesses / Tree Care for Residential and Commercial Properties / What Our Arborists Handle |
| Avoid DIY Risks with Our Experienced Professionals | Skip the DIY Risks with Experienced Arborists / Why DIY Tree Work Costs More / Leave Risky Tree Work to Our Professionals |
| Schedule a Free Consultation Today | Book Your Free Arborist Consultation / Request a Free Tree Consultation / Get a Free Consultation From Our Arborists |
| Our Arborist Service Is Available Throughout [City] | Arborist Service Across [City] / Areas We Serve in [City] / Serving Every Part of [City] |
| Frequently Asked Questions | Keep as is (or "Arborist FAQs" if the person prefers) |

Also rotate across pages for the same client:
- **H1 DIY angle:** bad pruning cuts, topping, wounds and disease entry, root damage from DIY digging or grinding, misjudged limb weight.
- **Why Choose H3 set:** don't repeat the exact same 4 USP headings on two pages for the same client. Reword them, or pick a different relevant USP.
- **Areas:** each location page uses that location's own areas.

## QA (in addition to the tone-and-qa-rules.md checklist)

1. Exact phrase "arborist service in [City], [ST]" appears in the meta description and the H1 body
2. No "study," "training," "train," or "trained"; no "dead" in Tree Removal; no "regular" in Tree Trimming
3. No phone number in the consultation section; brand name appears exactly once there
4. No business name in FAQ answers
5. Area descriptions don't repeat the area name and run 10 to 12 words
6. Service links point to this client's URLs (or are omitted and flagged), never another client's site
7. Section word counts: H1 40, Why Choose intro 30, USP H3s 20 to 25, Services intro 20, service H3s 20 to 22, DIY 100 to 120, Consultation 40, Areas intro 20, FAQ answers 22 to 25
8. No unconfirmed claims (certified, licensed, insured, flat pricing) beyond what the client profile supports
