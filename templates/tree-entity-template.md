# Tree Service Entity Page Template

Use this template for a "tree service near [Location Name]" page (an "entity" page) for a tree client. The person gives a location name and some details about the place. The place is a small landmark or area inside a city (a church, school, station, stadium, studio, park, neighborhood, subdivision, business district, or other community landmark).

Focus on the landmark given, not the city. The city and state can come up, but they stay secondary throughout.

These pages are written in the hundreds with the same outline. To avoid repetition, localize every section with the details the person gives and with research, without sounding forced. Work the person's unique details into the outline.

**WORD COUNT LIMITS ARE HARD LIMITS.** Count every section before finishing. Never go over.

Apply the client's files (`about.md`, `customer-persona.md`, `pipeline-notes.md`) for the business name, services, USPs, equipment, and base address.

## Which Variant to Use

| Situation | Variant |
|---|---|
| One entity page for a client (default) | **A. Regular** |
| A request for several entity pages for the same business | **B. Multi-Page** for every page in that request |
| The page is for project manager Rakib | **C. Rakib** (wins over A and B) |

Check the client's Entity Page Tracker in `pipeline-notes.md` before writing. Do not repeat the H1 title angle, H2 wording, opening sentence pattern, or tool list order used in the client's last several entity pages.

## Variant A: Regular

### Structure

- Meta description (no heading), 25 words
- H1: Location overview, then a natural slide into local trees and their care, 120 words
- H2: Proper tree service provided by us (modify the heading), 100 words
- H2: Our customized approach with modern tools (modify the heading), 100 words
- H2: Driving directions (company name in the heading)

### Part 1: Meta Description (25 words)

- Bold the label: **meta description:**
- Start with "Get".
- Must include and bold "**tree service near [full Location Name]**".
- No other service names. No phone number.
- Mention benefits of our service instead.

### Part 2: H1 Section (120 words maximum, 4 paragraphs)

H1: "Tree Service Near [Location Name]" plus something about the place's significance (e.g., "Tree Service Near Columbia Co School District: Preserving Local Education Roots").

- Write about this place first. If it's a church, stadium, school, studio, or neighborhood, focus on that.
- Say where it is located, then why it is popular and significant: its history, impact, and influence on the community.
- Cover what it is best known for and what living around it is like (the surrounding communities).
- Briefly mention the city or town it is in. Keep that short.
- Mention the trees common in the area.
- Then slide naturally into tree topics: local trees, the challenges they face, and how to care for them. The shift from the place to trees must not feel abrupt or forced.
- Don't mention the business name in this section.
- Research the place on the SERP, Reddit, and Facebook, plus the person's details.

### Part 3: H2, Tree Service Provided by Us (100 words)

Heading idea: "How [Location] Has Access to Quality Tree Service." Modify it for better user experience.

- Put the brand name in the very first line.
- Promote the business in the place named in the heading. Say we are a local team.
- Mention our services and the benefits that convince a reader to hire us (from client files).
- Mention nearby neighborhoods and other local references.
- Say we offer customized care and follow a customized approach based on this place's features and weather.
- Localize with local tree names, soil type, and common problems we solve.
- Mention how the services benefit the property, and the tools we use for the best results. Write "We use..." rather than "Pole saws and chainsaws do...".
- Don't start any sentence with "You get". Don't write "we handle" anywhere.
- Sentences neither too long nor too short. Professional tone.

### Part 4: H2, Our Customized Approach with Modern Tools (100 words)

Heading idea: "We Use the Right Tools for Your Yard." Modify it for better user experience.

- Mention the business name once.
- Localize as much as possible: geological features, soil, and weather. Say how our team customizes the approach to suit them.
- Explain how this keeps the work safe and protects the environment.
- Include 5 to 6 tools and equipment we have (from client files where listed) and how they give a better result.
- Say we try to avoid heavy equipment and work manually, and bring advanced equipment only when no other option works.
- Don't repeat information from earlier sections.

### Part 5: Driving Directions

See **Driving Directions Rules** below.

## Variant C: Rakib

Use when the person says the page is for Rakib. Ideal sentence length: **8 to 12 words.** Word limits are hard limits.

### Structure

- Meta description (no heading), 155 characters (23 to 25 words)
- H1: Location overview while connecting to tree service needs, 130 words maximum, 2 to 3 paragraphs
- H2: Proper tree service provided by us (modify the heading), 120 words
- H2: Driving directions (company name in the heading)

No tools H2 in this variant.

### Part 1: Meta Description (155 characters, 23 to 25 words)

- Bold the label: **meta description:**
- Start with an action verb (Get, Find, etc.).
- Must include and bold "**tree service near [Location Name]**".
- No other service names. No phone number. Mention benefits instead.
- Never use "mature trees".
- Example shape: "Get expert tree service near... We offer free quotes, bring advanced equipment, and ensure affordable pricing. Call today." (Shape only. "ensure" is a banned word.)

### Part 2: H1 Section (130 words maximum, 2 to 3 paragraphs)

H1 is exactly: "Tree Service Near [Location Name]"

- In the first 1 to 2 paragraphs, write only about this place. First say where it is located, then its impact.
- Cover history, impact, influence on the community, what it is known for, what living around it is like, and the common trees.
- Then slide naturally into tree topics: local trees, their challenges, and how to care for them.
- Localize by explaining our expertise working in these conditions and solving these problems, without naming the business.
- Research on the SERP, Reddit, and Facebook, plus the person's details.

### Part 3: H2, Tree Service Provided by Us (120 words)

Same rules as Variant A Part 3 (brand name in first line, local team, services, benefits, neighborhoods, customized approach based on the place's features and weather, tools for best results, no "You get" starts, no "we handle").

### Part 4: Driving Directions

See **Driving Directions Rules** below.

Also apply the Rakib rules in `CLAUDE.md`: plain, precise wording, no claim the client files or research don't support, no filler, and an extra pass for banned words and awkward phrasing.

## Driving Directions Rules (all variants)

H2 includes the company name (e.g., "Driving Directions From Gateway Tree Service," "Route to [Location] from [Business Name]").

The person pastes driving directions from Google Maps, and sometimes a Google Maps embed `<iframe>`.

1. If an iframe is given, place it right under the H2, unchanged.
2. One conversational starter line before the bullets. Mention the brand name here (once in the section). Example: "Barto Stumps and Tree Service is only 9 miles away, which takes about 15 minutes for us to arrive."
3. 4 to 6 bullets, 10 to 15 words each.
4. We are the ones driving to the place, not the reader. The bullets must not read as instructions for the customer or as a manual. Keep them conversational but as easy to follow as Google Maps steps.
5. "We," "our," and "us" are allowed, but don't start every bullet with "We." Vary starts (Our crew, The team, Our trucks, From there, etc.).
6. Keep road names and turns exactly as the pasted directions give them.
7. End with a short line: if this shortest road is busy or blocked, we take other routes we know well.
8. If no directions are given, ask the person to paste them before writing this section.

## Writing Instructions (all variants, all sections)

### 1. Voice
Confident, professional, and reassuring, like an experienced local expert. No exaggeration or hype ("best in the world"). Emphasize experience, safety, and reliability.

### 2. Reading Level
- 8th-grade maximum (Hemingway).
- Start sentences mostly with a subject, not a phrase.
- Short sentences, 10 to 15 words (8 to 12 for Rakib).
- Active voice, present tense.

### 3. Perspective
Second person ("you," "your property"). Start some sentences with "You." Write like a top copywriter.

### 4. Emotional Anchors
Safety, trust, professionalism, and cleanup.

### 5. Constraints and Precision Rules
- No em dashes (locked).
- No comma + -ing verb constructions.
- No fluff phrases ("we are passionate," "we care deeply").
- Avoid adverbs. Avoid very, super, way, actually. Use strong verbs and compound adjectives (nerve-wracking, storm-battered, root-heaved). Skip any example adjective that is on the banned list (e.g., "daunting").
- Usually one comma per sentence. With two commas, cut the middle or split into two sentences.
- No redundant or filler words.
  - Bad: We simultaneously left the building at the same time.
  - Good: We left the building at the same time.
  - Bad: Our Demolition Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care, precision, and complete debris removal, leaving your property clean and ready for use.
  - Good: Our Demolition Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care and complete debris removal. We leave your property clean and ready for use.
- Never use "anchor" or "sits".
- Apply the global Banned Words List in `templates/tone-and-qa-rules.md` plus the client's banned words.

## What Applies from tone-and-qa-rules.md

- Applies: Voice, reading level, banned words, no superlative or guarantee claims beyond the client profile, no two consecutive sentences sharing a starting word.
- Does not apply: the tree Required Terms minimums, FAQ rules, H3 rules, and the 1% keyword density cap. These pages run about 350 to 450 words, and the required placements alone (meta, H1, brand name in each H2) push "tree service" and the location name past 1%. Instead, don't repeat "tree service," "tree care," "expert," or the location name beyond the required placements; use "our crew," "the team," or "the area" in their place. Run `python3 scripts/keyword_density.py <page.md>` and report the numbers. Use "arborist" only where it fits naturally and the client supports it.

## QA Checklist (run before finishing)

1. Correct variant used (Rakib > Multi-Page > Regular).
2. Every section within its word limit (count, don't estimate). Meta at 25 words (Rakib: 155 characters, 23 to 25 words).
3. Bolded "meta description:" label and bolded "tree service near [Location Name]".
4. H1 says nothing about the business name. Place first, city secondary, smooth slide to trees (Variant B: species only, no problems).
5. Brand name in the first line of the service H2. Brand name once in the tools H2. Brand name once in the directions section and in its H2.
6. No em dashes, no comma + -ing verbs, no "anchor," "sits," "You get" starts, or "we handle." Rakib: no "mature trees."
7. No banned words (global + client).
8. No two consecutive sentences share a starting word. Directions bullets don't all start with "We."
9. No extra repeats of "tree service," "tree care," "expert," or the location name beyond required placements. Density script run and numbers reported.
10. Headings differ from the client's recent entity pages (Entity Page Tracker).
