# Painting Location Page Template

Common page outline for painting clients (e.g., In & Outdoor Solution). Same structure as the hood cleaning location page (`hood-cleaning-location-page-template.md`), modified for painting. Replace [City, ST] and [Business Name] for each page.

Apply `tone-and-qa-rules.md` for tone, sentence length, banned words, and punctuation. The tree-only parts of that file (Service Definitions, tree Required Terms like "arborist" and "tree surgeons") do not apply.

The person usually supplies local information with the request (local building or property types, neighborhoods, codes). Work it in naturally throughout the page.

## Page Structure

### Meta Description
24 words. Start with "Get."

### H1: Painting in [City, ST] [40 words]
First talk about what happens to paint and surfaces over time (fading, peeling, cracking, moisture damage, mildew). Then introduce our service as the solution, and mention our features or USPs.

### H2: Reliable Local Painters at Your Service [100 words]
Localize the content. Mention local home and building types, how the local weather affects paint, the problems property owners see, and how our team solves them. Also mention specific rules (for example, lead-safe practices for pre-1978 homes, HOA color rules, permits for commercial work), and that we know them and comply with them.

### H2: Our Painting Services
- First, 30 words saying we offer full painting solutions (do not use the banned word "comprehensive").
- Then 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences for each service. Say exactly what we do and the benefits.
- H3s: use the client's service list, for example:
  - Interior Painting
  - Exterior Painting
  - Pressure Washing
  - Epoxy Coatings

### H2: Why Choose [Business Name]
- First, 20 to 22 words saying the community trusts us.
- Then 4 USPs that will convince customers to hire us, related to painting.
- Each USP is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do.

### H2: Our Working Process
- First, mention the business name and say we follow a safe method.
- Then 4 steps related to painting.
- Each step is an H3 of 3 to 4 words, followed by 22 to 25 words in 2 sentences explaining exactly what we do in that step.

### H2: We Bring Modern Painting Service Throughout [City]
- First, 20 words saying we serve every side of the town.
- Then 10 to 12 words for each local area (from the local information provided). Talk about the homes and buildings around each area.

### H2: Frequently Asked Questions
Precise answer to each question in 22 to 25 words. Give the direct answer first, then promote our service.

- How often should a home be repainted in [City]?
- How long does a painting project take?
- How much does painting in [City] cost?

Note: the FAQ questions and H2 wording above were adapted from the hood cleaning outline. Adjust them if the person gives different ones.
