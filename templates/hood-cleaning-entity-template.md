# Hood Cleaning Entity Page Template

Use this template for a "hood cleaning service near [Location Name]" page. The person gives a location name and some details about the place. The place is a small landmark or area inside a city (a studio, stadium, church, school, station, neighborhood, or other local landmark).

Focus on the landmark given, not the city. The city and state can be discussed, but they stay secondary.

Apply the client's files (`about.md`, `customer-persona.md`, `pipeline-notes.md`) for business details, services, and base address.

## Final Structure Summary

- Meta Description (no heading)
- H1: Location overview
- H2: Proper hood cleaning service provided by us (modify the heading)
- H2: Driving directions

## Part 1: Meta Description

Write a meta description of 155 characters. Bold the term "meta description:" at the start.

- Start with an action verb, like get, find, etc.
- Must include the term "hood cleaning service near"
- Must mention and bold "hood cleaning service near" + location name
- Don't mention other service names
- No phone number
- Mention the benefits of our services instead

Example of a good meta description:

> Get expert hood cleaning service near... We offer free quotes, bring advanced equipment, and ensure affordable pricing. Call today.

## Part 2: H1 Section

H1: "Hood Cleaning Service Near [Location Name]," given a good title that mentions the location name and something about its significance.

- 100 words maximum, in 2 to 3 paragraphs. Don't write more than 100 words.
- In the first 1 to 2 paragraphs, write only about this location. First say where it is located, then its impact. Use the information provided. Don't mention the business name here.
- Talk about the specific location first (studio, stadium, church, or other place).
- Cover:
  - Its history, impact, and influence on the community
  - What it is most known for, and what it is like to live around it (the surrounding communities)
  - What types of commercial kitchens are common in the area
- Then slide naturally into commercial kitchen topics: local restaurants, the challenges they face, and how to care for their hoods. The transition from location to commercial kitchen hoods must not be abrupt or forced.
- Gather information from SERP, Reddit, and Facebook as well.
- No em dashes (locked rule).
- Aim for 8th-grade readability.

## Writing Instructions (all sections)

### 1. Voice
Confident, professional, and reassuring, like an experienced local expert. Avoid exaggeration or hype (e.g., "best in the world"). Emphasize experience, safety, and reliability.

### 2. Reading Level
8th-grade maximum (Hemingway).

- Start sentences mostly with a subject instead of phrases
- Short sentences of 10 to 15 words
- Active voice, present tense

### 3. Perspective
Second person ("you," "your property") to make it relatable. Start some sentences with "You" and write like a top-class copywriter.

### 4. Emotional Anchors
Safety, trust, professionalism, and cleanup.

### 5. Constraints and Precision Rules

- Avoid fluff phrases like "we are passionate" or "we care deeply"
- Avoid adverbs whenever possible (adverbs usually mean weak verbs). Avoid words like very, super, way, actually, etc.
- Use strong verbs and compound adjectives. For example, for fear: bloodcurdling, soul-sucking, anxiety-inducing, nerve-wracking, overwhelming, frightening, scary, petrifying.
- Simple, short sentences, usually one comma. If a sentence needs two commas, cut the middle or make two sentences.
- Avoid redundant words and filler words.
- Avoid the terms "anchor" and "sits"

Examples:

- Bad: We simultaneously left the building at the same time.
- Good: We left the building at the same time.
- Bad: The teacher explains the lesson in a very clear and concise manner, answering questions.
- Good: The teacher explains the lesson clearly and answers all questions right away.
- Bad: Our Demolition Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care, precision, and complete debris removal, leaving your property clean and ready for use.
- Good: Our Demolition Company in Pittsburgh handles each project with care, precision, and complete debris removal. We leave your property clean and ready for use.

- 100 words

- Mention neighborhoods and nearby communities
- Don't write sentences that are too long or too short. Sound professional.
- Mention that we offer customized care, and localize the content using the location details provided
- Mention how these services benefit the reader, and the tools we use for the best results
- Say that we follow a customized approach based on this location's features and weather
- Say that our service follows local codes and other regulations required in the area
- Avoid:
  - Em dashes
  - Comma + -ing verbs
  - Starting sentences with "You get"
  - Saying "we handle" in any sentence

## Part 4: Driving Directions Section

Add an H2 that shows the route we take from our base to the destination. Mention the company name in the H2.

The person pastes driving directions from Google Maps. Convert them into conversational instructions.

- A one-line starter before the bullets. Example: "Barto Stumps and Tree Service is only x miles away, which may take x minutes for us to arrive."
- 4 to 6 bullet points
- Each bullet contains 10 to 15 words
- Avoid comma + -ing verbs and em dashes
- It must not sound like a manual. Keep it conversational.
- We are the hood cleaning company driving to the destination, not the reader. It must not sound like the customer is being asked to follow the route.
- "We," "our," and "us" are allowed, but don't start every sentence with "we."
- Keep the instructions like Google Maps directions: short and easy to follow.
- Mention the brand name once.
- After the bullets, end with a short sentence saying that if this road, the shortest route, is busy or blocked, we can take other routes we are familiar with.

## Important

Focus on the exact place given, whether it's a school, station, church, or neighborhood, not the entire city. The city and state can be discussed, but they are secondary.
