# Highline Tree Service

## GBP Product: Crane Service

**Product name:** Crane Service

**Description:**

Highline Tree Service uses a crane to remove large trees near your home, garage, or power lines. Our climbers attach rigging to each section, and the crane operator takes the weight before the cut. That hold lets us swing the section clear and lower it to the ground. Your roof, fence, and garden stay out of the drop zone. The crane also reaches leaning, dead, or storm-damaged trees that are too unstable to climb. Our team handles this work for property owners in Lakeview, MI, and the surrounding towns. Request an estimate, and we will walk you through the setup before the work starts.
