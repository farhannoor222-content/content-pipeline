# Rooted In Texas Tree Care

## GBP Product: Tree Service

**Product name:** Tree Service

**Description:**

Trees on your property can threaten your roof, crowd power lines, or die from disease long before the damage shows. Our experienced arborists walk the property with you, name each problem, and explain the fix and the cost in plain terms.

Rooted In Texas Tree Care then trims, removes, grinds stumps, cables weak limbs, and clears lots, using the safest method that fits each tree and your property. You get a free estimate, upfront pricing, and no hidden fees, so the cost never changes mid-job. Our satisfaction guarantee backs the finished work.

## GBP Product: Arborist Service

**Product name:** Arborist Service

**Description:**

A tree that looks fine can hide root rot, cankers, or oak wilt that spread through the wood. Our experienced arborists inspect the leaves, bark, trunk, root flare, and soil around the base, then identify the cause of any decline.

From that diagnosis we recommend the lightest fix first, whether that is pruning, cabling a weak limb, or removal when saving the tree is not realistic. You get a clear plan for your tree's care, upfront pricing, and no hidden fees. Ask Rooted In Texas Tree Care for a free estimate.
