# Midwest Tree

## GBP Service: Mulching

**Service name:** Mulching

**Description:**

Bare soil dries out and invites weeds around your trees. Midwest Tree spreads mulch in a wide ring at the right depth and keeps it back from the trunk, so roots stay cool and moist. You get tidier beds and healthier trees. Cleanup is included.

## GBP Service: Tree Fertilization

**Service name:** Tree Fertilization

**Description:**

Yellowing leaves and thin growth often point to low nutrients in the soil. Our team applies fertilizer around the root zone, so the tree grows fuller and handles stress better. Midwest Tree gives you a free written estimate first.

## GBP Service: Cabling & Bracing

**Service name:** Cabling & Bracing

**Description:**

Weak, split, or heavy limbs can fail in wind and snow. Our team installs steel cables and braces that share the load between limbs. You keep a mature tree standing. Midwest Tree explains the plan with transparent pricing.

## GBP Service: Canopy Thinning

**Service name:** Canopy Thinning

**Description:**

Midwest Tree thins dense canopies by cutting selected inner branches back to the trunk or a larger limb, so wind and sunlight pass through. You get a lighter tree that keeps its shape and a brighter lawn. Ask for a free written estimate.
