# Midwest Tree

## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

An old stump hides trip hazards in your lawn, blocks your mower, and sends up sprouts around its edges. Midwest Tree uses a stump grinder to cut the stump into chips and take it below the soil line to the depth you agree on.

That method breaks up the main wood without digging a big hole, so your yard stays level. Our team cleans up the chips when grinding ends, and cleanup is included in the work. You get a free written estimate, transparent pricing, and ground ready for new grass or a replanted tree.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

Storm winds can drop a tree or heavy limb onto your roof, driveway, or fence in minutes, and more damage often hides underneath. Our team sizes up the lean and the weight first, then makes the scene safe before any cutting starts, so nobody gets hurt.

We cut the fallen tree into sections and lift each piece clear with ropes and rigging, so heavy wood stays off the damage. Cleanup is included, and our free written estimate explains the plan and cost with transparent pricing. Call Midwest Tree when a tree comes down.
