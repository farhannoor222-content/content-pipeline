## About the Company

Midwest Tree is a locally owned Tree Service based in Lenexa, Kansas, proudly serving homeowners and businesses throughout Johnson County, including Olathe, Overland Park, and Shawnee.

Our company was built on a simple belief: tree care should be done safely, professionally, and with complete accountability. From routine tree trimming to complex removals and emergency response, every job is approached with careful planning and attention to detail.

We are committed to protecting your property, strengthening your trees, and delivering tree care services you can rely on year after year.
