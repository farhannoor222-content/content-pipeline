# Borders Tree Service

## GBP Product: Tree Trimming

**Product name:** Tree Trimming

**Description:**

Borders Tree Service trims overgrown limbs that scrape your roof, drop debris in your gutters, and crowd the power lines near your home. We cut each limb back to a healthy branch, so the cut heals cleanly and the tree keeps its natural shape. Our team works from the ground and from the tree, and we haul away the cuttings when we finish. You get safer clearance around your house, more light for your lawn, and a tree that grows stronger instead of weaker. Ask us which limbs should come off before storm season arrives.

## GBP Product: Stump Removal

**Product name:** Stump Removal

**Description:**

An old stump blocks new landscaping, hides trip hazards in the grass, and keeps sprouting at its edges. Our team digs around the base, cuts the main roots loose, and lifts the whole stump out of the ground. That method clears the roots as well as the wood, so you do not fight new sprouts or buried wood where you want to plant. We haul the stump away and level the hole. You get open ground you can replant, build on, or mow with ease. Call Borders Tree Service to schedule your stump removal.

## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

Mowing around a stump wastes your time, and the stump itself is a hazard for kids and pets. Borders Tree Service runs a stump grinder that spins a cutting wheel across the wood and turns the stump into chips, taking it below the soil line to the depth you agree on. That leaves the roots to rot away underground, so you skip the digging and the big hole. We clean up the chips when the grinding ends. You get level, mowable ground ready for new grass or a replanted tree.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

Storms can drop a tree or a heavy limb across your roof, driveway, or fence in minutes. Our team answers urgent calls, sizes up the danger, and makes the scene safe first. Then Borders Tree Service cuts the fallen tree into sections and lifts each one clear with ropes and rigging, which keeps heavy wood from landing on damage that is already there. We clear the debris from your property afterward. You get open access to your home, less risk to your family, and a clear plan for what repairs come next.

## GBP Product: Hazardous Tree Removal

**Product name:** Hazardous Tree Removal

**Description:**

Dead, leaning, or split trees can fall without warning and hit your house, your car, or a neighbor's fence. Our team inspects the trunk, roots, and lean to judge how the tree will move. We then take it down section by section, using ropes and rigging to lower each piece, so heavy wood never crashes onto your roof. Calling Borders Tree Service early means you deal with the hazard on your schedule, not during the next storm. You get a safer yard, a lower risk to your family, and a hazard gone before it fails.
