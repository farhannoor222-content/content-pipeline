# Borders Tree Service

## GBP Product: Tree Trimming

**Product name:** Tree Trimming

**Description:**

Borders Tree Service trims overgrown limbs that scrape your roof, drop debris in your gutters, and crowd nearby power lines. We cut each limb back to a healthy branch, so the cut heals cleanly and the tree keeps its natural shape.

Our team works from the ground and from the tree, then hauls away the cuttings so you do not bag brush yourself. You get safer clearance around your house, more light for your lawn and garden, and a stronger tree before storm season arrives.

## GBP Product: Stump Removal

**Product name:** Stump Removal

**Description:**

An old stump blocks new landscaping, hides trip hazards in the grass, and keeps sprouting at its edges. Our team digs around the base, cuts the main roots loose, and lifts the whole stump out of the ground.

That method clears the roots as well as the wood, so you can plant or build on open ground without fighting new sprouts. We haul the stump away and level the hole, so the spot is ready for your next project. Call Borders Tree Service to schedule your stump removal.

## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

Mowing around a stump wastes your time, and the stump is a hazard for kids and pets. Borders Tree Service runs a stump grinder that turns the stump into chips and takes it below the soil line to the depth you agree on.

That leaves the roots to rot underground, so you skip the digging and the big hole. We clean up the chips when the grinding ends, and you get level, mowable ground ready for new grass or a replanted tree.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

Storms can drop a tree or a heavy limb across your roof, driveway, or fence in minutes. Our team answers urgent calls, sizes up the danger, and makes the scene safe before any cutting starts.

Then Borders Tree Service cuts the fallen tree into sections and lifts each one clear with ropes and rigging, which keeps heavy wood off existing damage. We clear the debris from your property afterward, so you get open access to your home and a clear plan for repairs.

## GBP Product: Hazardous Tree Removal

**Product name:** Hazardous Tree Removal

**Description:**

Dead, leaning, or split trees can fall without warning and hit your house, your car, or a neighbor's fence. Our team inspects the trunk, roots, and lean, then takes the tree down section by section with ropes and rigging.

That method lowers each piece to the ground, so heavy wood never crashes onto your roof. Calling Borders Tree Service early lets you handle the hazard on your schedule, not during the next storm. You get a safer yard and a lower risk to your family.
