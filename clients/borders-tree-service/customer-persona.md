# Borders Tree Service Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview (60 words)

The ideal client for Borders Tree Service is a property owner in Sutton County or the surrounding Edwards Plateau who prioritizes the long-term health and safety of their landscape. These individuals often manage expansive residential lots or commercial properties where mature trees are vital assets. They value professional expertise and clear, dependable communication to protect their investment from the unpredictable Texas elements.

* Geographic Focus: Primary residence or business located in Sonora, TX, with secondary targets in Big Lake, Ozona, and Eldorado.
* Property Type: Owners of single-family homes with established oak or pecan trees, as well as local commercial facility managers.
* Value Alignment: Seeks an experienced arborist rather than a general laborer to ensure precision and property safety.
* Problem Awareness: Concerned about storm damage, encroaching branches on power lines, or the aesthetic decline of overgrown trees.
* Service Requirement: Frequently requires a mix of routine maintenance (trimming) and technical dismantling (tree removal).
* Safety Conscious: Prioritizes licensed and experienced professionals to mitigate the risks associated with large-scale cutting and grinding.
* Communication Style: Appreciates proactive updates, timely project completions, and post-job satisfaction checks.
* Maintenance Philosophy: Views tree care as a necessary preservation tactic for property value and "bringing the property to life."
* Urgency Level: Includes both proactive planners for seasonal trimming and high-stress callers needing emergency response.
* Decision Criteria: Relies on the company’s decade-long track record and the presence of a specialized arborist over the lowest bid.
