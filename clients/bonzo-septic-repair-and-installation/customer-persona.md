# Bonzo Septic Repair and Installation Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview (60 words)

Bonzo Septic Repair and Installation caters to homeowners and property managers in the Pittsburgh area who prioritize reliability, safety, and affordability. Clients typically need urgent septic system repair or long-term maintenance. They value licensed, insured professionals offering free estimates and guarantees. The typical client seeks prompt, high-quality services in residential, small multi-family, or light commercial properties.
