## GBP Product: Tree Trimming

**Product name:** Tree Trimming

**Description:**

Overgrown limbs rub your roof, crowd power lines, and drop debris in your gutters. David Kennedy Tree & Landscaping cuts each limb back to the right branch collar, so the tree heals cleanly and keeps its natural shape. That trimming opens clearance around your home and lets more light reach your lawn. We haul away the cuttings when the job ends. Our free same-day estimate explains the work and cost first, and our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee backs the result.

## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

A leftover stump gets in the way of your mower and creates a trip hazard for kids and pets. Our team grinds the stump into chips with a stump grinder and takes it down to the depth you agree on. That depth leaves level ground for new grass or a replanted tree. We clean up the chips and debris when we finish. Request a free same-day estimate, and our licensed, insured team will walk you through the process.

**Product name:** Stump Removal

**Description:**

Stump removal pulls the whole stump and its main roots out of your yard. Our team digs around the base, cuts the roots loose, and lifts the stump out of the ground. That clears space you can regrade, replant, or build on. Our team hauls the stump away, so you do not have to deal with the debris. We are licensed and insured, and our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee covers the job. Ask for a free same-day estimate to see the cost first.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

Storm winds can drop a limb or an entire tree onto your roof, driveway, or fence. David Kennedy Tree & Landscaping offers emergency service for these urgent hazards. Our team cuts the fallen tree into manageable sections, so heavy wood stays off the damage and off you. We then clear the debris from your property. Call us when a tree threatens your home, and we will explain the plan before we start. Our team is licensed and insured, and our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee covers the work.
