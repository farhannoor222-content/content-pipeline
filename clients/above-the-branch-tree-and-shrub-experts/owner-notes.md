## What Affects Price
- Cost of hauling wood. Chips are usually free at most nearby mulch yards. If wood goes by truck, Mike said that runs $500 to $2,000 extra (transcript says "lock truck", likely a log truck; confirm).
- Access to the tree and where it sits.
- Whether a crane is needed.
- What's around the tree: power lines, structures, pools.
- Stump grinding options: leave the grindings, or remove them and add topsoil and seed for a full restoration.
- Permit costs, such as a $50 township permit.

## What Homeowners Get Wrong
- "The cheapest price isn't always the best." Mike's main point.
- Some think any big job needs sky-high pricing. The crane job showed fair pricing is possible when it's scheduled and planned properly.
- Trimming oaks and pines in extreme heat stresses them. Cooler months are better.
- Hazardous trees can't wait for a season. Remove them year-round.
