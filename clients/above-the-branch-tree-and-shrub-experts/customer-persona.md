## Target Client Overview

Above The Branch Tree & Shrub Experts primarily serves established Southampton homeowners who value safety, property protection, and dependable workmanship. These customers are often busy professionals, longtime residents, retirees, and property managers who need tree removal, trimming, stump work, or urgent storm response.
