# Aguilar Lawn and Landscape

## GBP Service: Dead Tree Removal

**Service name:** Dead Tree Removal

**Description:**

Dead trees drop limbs without warning and can fall on your house or fence. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape removes them section by section with ropes and rigging, so heavy wood never lands on your property. You get a safer yard and room for new planting.

## GBP Service: Hazardous Tree Removal

**Service name:** Hazardous Tree Removal

**Description:**

A leaning, split, or storm-weakened tree puts your home at risk. Our team inspects the trunk and roots, then lowers the tree in sections. You get the hazard gone on your schedule. Call Aguilar Lawn and Landscape to plan the removal.

## GBP Service: Fallen Tree Removal

**Service name:** Fallen Tree Removal

**Description:**

Aguilar Lawn and Landscape clears fallen trees from your yard, driveway, or fence line. We cut each trunk into pieces and haul the wood away. You get your property back and less cleanup to do.

## GBP Service: Tree Planting

**Service name:** Tree Planting

**Description:**

New trees add shade and curb appeal for years. Our team digs the hole, sets each tree at the right depth, and backfills with soil so the roots settle. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape gives every tree a healthy start.

## GBP Service: Landscaping Design

**Service name:** Landscaping Design

**Description:**

Every yard starts with a plan. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape lays out beds, plants, and walkways around your home's size and sun exposure. You get a design that fits your space and a clear picture before work begins.

## GBP Service: Hardscaping

**Service name:** Hardscaping

**Description:**

Patios, walkways, and retaining walls give your yard lasting structure. Our team sets the base, lays each stone or paver, and levels the surface so it stays even. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape builds you a durable outdoor space.

## GBP Service: Grass Installation

**Service name:** Grass Installation

**Description:**

Bare patches and weeds make a lawn look worn. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape prepares the soil, lays new sod or seed, and waters it in so the grass takes root. You get a green, even lawn without the guesswork.

## GBP Service: Mulch Services

**Service name:** Mulch Services

**Description:**

Fresh mulch holds moisture, blocks weeds, and gives beds a clean edge. Our team spreads a layer around trees and beds at the right depth, so roots stay cool. You get tidier beds and healthier plants. Ask Aguilar Lawn and Landscape about mulch.
