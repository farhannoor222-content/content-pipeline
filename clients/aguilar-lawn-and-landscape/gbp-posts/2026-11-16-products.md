# Aguilar Lawn and Landscape

## GBP Product: Dead Tree Removal

**Product name:** Dead Tree Removal

**Description:**

Aguilar Lawn and Landscape removes dead trees before they drop limbs on your house, fence, or car. Dead wood turns brittle, so a strong wind can bring down a trunk or a heavy branch with no warning.

Our team inspects the trunk and roots, then cuts the tree down section by section, using ropes and rigging to lower each piece to the ground. That control keeps heavy wood off your roof, fence, and landscaping. You get a safer yard, a cleared site, and room for new planting when you are ready.

## GBP Product: Hazardous Tree Removal

**Product name:** Hazardous Tree Removal

**Description:**

A leaning, split, or storm-weakened tree can fall onto your home without warning. Our team inspects the trunk, the roots, and the lean to judge which way the weight will move and where to place each cut.

We then take the tree down section by section, lowering each piece with ropes and rigging so nothing crashes onto your roof. Calling Aguilar Lawn and Landscape early lets you fix the hazard on your schedule, not during the next storm. You get a safer yard and a lower risk to your family and property.

## GBP Product: Fallen Tree Removal

**Product name:** Fallen Tree Removal

**Description:**

When a tree falls across your yard, driveway, or fence, it blocks your access, strains your fence or roof, and can hide more damage underneath. Our team looks over the scene first, then cuts the trunk into manageable pieces with chainsaws.

We lift the pieces off the damage, haul the wood away, and clear the leaves, twigs, and small limbs left on your lawn. You get open access to your property again and a clear view of any repairs you need. Call Aguilar Lawn and Landscape when a tree comes down.

## GBP Product: Tree Planting

**Product name:** Tree Planting

**Description:**

New trees add shade and curb appeal, but a poorly planted tree struggles for years. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape digs each hole wide enough for the roots to spread and sets the tree at the right depth.

We backfill with soil, firm it around the trunk, and water the tree in well so the roots settle. That start helps the tree grow straight and steady instead of leaning or stalling in its first few years. You get a healthy tree that gives your yard shade, curb appeal, and value for years.

## GBP Product: Landscaping Design

**Product name:** Landscaping Design

**Description:**

Every yard starts with a plan, and a yard without one often ends up crowded, bare, or hard to maintain. Our team walks your whole property and studies its size, slope, drainage, and sun exposure.

Aguilar Lawn and Landscape then lays out beds, plants, and walkways that fit those conditions and your home. You see the plan before any digging starts, so you can adjust it to your budget and taste. You get an outdoor space that looks balanced, grows well, and takes less work to keep up each season.

## GBP Product: Hardscaping

**Product name:** Hardscaping

**Description:**

Patios, walkways, and retaining walls give your yard lasting structure, but they sink, crack, or shift when the base is weak. Our team excavates the area, grades it for drainage, and packs a firm gravel base before any stone goes down.

We lay each paver or stone, level the surface, and fill the joints so it stays even. That base work keeps the pieces from shifting after rain and freezing weather. You get a durable outdoor space you can use for years. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape builds it to fit your yard.

## GBP Product: Grass Installation

**Product name:** Grass Installation

**Description:**

Bare patches and weeds make a lawn look worn and tired, and they spread when the soil underneath is poor. Our team clears the old growth, loosens the soil, and levels the surface so water drains away.

Then we lay new sod or seed and water it in, so the grass takes root. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape handles the whole job from soil prep to the first watering. You get a thick, even lawn that holds up to foot traffic and fills in the bare spots so your yard looks finished.

## GBP Product: Mulch Services

**Product name:** Mulch Services

**Description:**

Fresh mulch holds moisture, blocks weeds, and gives your beds a clean edge, but a thick pile against a trunk can rot the bark and invite pests. Aguilar Lawn and Landscape spreads mulch around trees and beds at the right depth and keeps it back from the bark.

That method keeps roots cool in summer and soil damp between rains. Our team also clears the leaves and weeds from each bed first, so the mulch sits on clean ground and weeds do not push through. You get tidier beds and healthier plants.
