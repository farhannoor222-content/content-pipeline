# Aguilar Lawn and Landscape Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview

Homeowners and property managers in Shreveport, LA, who need expert tree care services. They value professional knowledge, safety, and fair pricing for maintenance and emergency tree-related issues. Their concerns include tree health, safety hazards, and property aesthetics.

## Demographics

* Age: 35-65
* Income: $50,000-$150,000
* Education: High school diploma to college degree
* Home Value: $150,000-$500,000
* Location: Shreveport, Bossier City, and nearby communities
* Property Type:
   * Historic homes: 20%
   * Modern single-family homes: 60%
   * High-end townhouses: 10%
   * Small multi-family properties: 10%

## Key Characteristics of the Customer

* Values expert knowledge and professional service
* Prioritizes safety for their family and property
* Prefers hassle-free, responsive service with clear communication
* Concerned about costs but willing to pay for reliability
* Lacks deep knowledge of tree health but wants clear recommendations

## Decision-Making Process

### 1. Initial Triggers

* Storm damage or fallen trees
* Tree disease, decay, or pest infestations
* Routine maintenance needs
* City or HOA compliance notices

### 2. Research Phase

* Online searches for local tree services
* Reading customer reviews and checking certifications
* Seeking recommendations from neighbors or community groups

### 3. Evaluation Criteria

* Experience, certification, and insurance
* Response time, availability, and professionalism
* Pricing transparency and service guarantees
* Customer testimonials and case studies

### 4. Quote Process

* Requests estimates from 2-3 providers
* Compares services, warranties, and work timelines
* Decides based on expertise, trust, and overall value

## Tree Data & Regional Challenges

### Common Tree Species in Shreveport

* Bald Cypress: Grows 50-70 feet, thrives in wet soil
* Loblolly Pine: Fast-growing, reaches 80-100 feet, common in Southern forests
* Bigleaf Magnolia: Large, glossy leaves, grows up to 60 feet
* Willow Oak: 40-75 feet, tolerates urban environments, provides dense shade
* Sweetgum: Grows 60-100 feet, produces spiky seed balls, popular in landscapes

### Weather & Seasonal Risks for Trees

* Spring: Pests (aphids, beetles), fungal infections, rapid growth requiring pruning
* Summer: Heat stress, risk of drought, increased limb breakage
* Fall: Leaf shedding, preparing trees for winter pruning
* Winter: Freezing temperatures, ice damage, storm-related risks
