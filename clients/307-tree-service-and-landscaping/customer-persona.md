# 307 Tree Service and Landscaping Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview

Homeowners and property managers in Basin, WY and nearby towns who need tree services. They want reliable, affordable tree care from local experts. Most clients contact the company for tree removal, trimming, stump grinding, or emergency services after storms.

## Common Tree Names

* Cottonwood
* Blue Spruce
* Ponderosa Pine
* Aspen
* Juniper
* Box Elder

## Common Tree Problems

* Winter storm damage
* Drought stress
* Disease (pine beetle, fungal infections)
* Overgrown branches near structures
* Root damage
* Dead/dying trees

## Demographics

* Age: 35-65
* Income: $50,000-$120,000
* Education: High school to college degree
* Home Value: $200,000-$450,000
* Location: Basin, Cody, Powell, Greybull, Worland, Thermopolis
* Property Type:
   * Historic homes (15%)
   * Modern single-family homes (65%)
   * High-end townhouses (5%)
   * Small multi-family properties (15%)

## Key Customer Characteristics

* Values work from local businesses
* Concerned about property safety
* Price-conscious but willing to pay for quality
* Prefers clear, direct communication
* Expects professional service and proper cleanup

## Decision-Making Process

### 1. Initial Trigger Events

* Storm-damaged trees
* Dead/diseased trees posing safety risks
* Overgrown branches near power lines/structures
* Stumps interfering with landscaping plans
* Tree roots damaging foundations/sidewalks

### 2. Research Phase

* Google searches for "tree removal in Basin, WY"
* Checks reviews on Google Business Profile
* Asks neighbors for recommendations
* Compares quotes from 2-3 companies

### 3. Evaluation Criteria

* Pricing transparency
* Proof of insurance
* Local reputation
* Response time
* Previous work examples

### 4. Quote Process

* Expects free on-site estimates
* Wants clear breakdown of costs
* Prefers written quotes
* Makes decision within 1-2 weeks for non-emergencies
