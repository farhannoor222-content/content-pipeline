# K&C Rochester Tree and Landscape Customer Profile

## K&C Rochester Tree and Landscape Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview

Core customers are Rochester homeowners with properties in established neighborhoods like Strafford Woods, Brandon Heights, and near downtown. Most have lived in their homes 10+ years and have mature trees that need regular care.

## Demographics

* Age Range: 35-65
* Average Income: $75,000-$125,000
* Education: Mix of college and trade backgrounds
* Work: Local employers (Frisbie Memorial, local schools, manufacturing)
* Home Value: $300,000-$500,000
* Property Size: 0.25-1 acre lots
* Household: 60% married, 40% single
* Children: Mix of young families and empty nesters

## Individual Overview

Typical customers own homes built between 1960-2000 with 4-8 mature trees. They work full-time and need flexible scheduling. Most became interested in tree care after storm damage or neighbor recommendations.
