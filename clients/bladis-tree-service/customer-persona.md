# Bladi's Tree Service Customer Profile

* Bladi's Tree Service: Detailed Customer Persona

## Target Client Overview (60 words)

Bladi's typical client is a homeowner aged 35-65 in the Dallas-Fort Worth suburbs, owning a single-family property with mature trees. They earn middle to upper-middle income, value safety and curb appeal, and seek licensed, insured professionals. Triggers include storm damage, HOA notices, or routine maintenance. They research online, prioritize trust signals, and expect transparent pricing with fast, reliable communication.

## Demographics

* Age: 35-65 years old, homeowners with established properties
* Income: $70,000 to $150,000 annual household income
* Education: Bachelor's degree or higher, professional or skilled trades
* Home Value: $250,000 to $600,000 typical property valuation
* Location: Arlington, Grand Prairie, Fort Worth, Mansfield, Southlake areas

## Key Characteristics of the Customer

* Time-constrained professionals preferring online booking and quick communication
* Risk-averse, prioritizing licensed and insured service providers

## Decision-Making Process

1. Initial Trigger Events:
* Storm damage causing fallen limbs or leaning trees
* HOA violation notices regarding overgrown or hazardous trees
* Seasonal maintenance needs before storm season approaches
* Home sale preparation requiring improved curb appeal
* Visible signs of tree disease, decay, or dead branches
2. Research Phase:
* Google search for "tree service near me" on mobile
* Review Google Business Profile ratings and customer feedback
* Check Nextdoor and Facebook groups for local recommendations
* Compare company websites for services, pricing, and credentials
* Look for licensing, insurance badges, and years in business
3. Evaluation Criteria:
* Number and quality of five-star Google reviews
* Licensing and insurance verification clearly displayed
* Response time for emergency or quote requests
* Transparency of pricing and absence of hidden fees
* Local presence and familiarity with area-specific tree species
4. Quote Process:
* Request free, no-obligation estimate via phone or online form
* Expect prompt scheduling for on-site property assessment
* Compare 2-3 quotes for non-urgent jobs only
* Skip comparison entirely during emergency situations
* Look for itemized pricing covering removal, cleanup, and disposal
