## Specific Tree Species Knowledge and Concerns

* Tulip poplar: Tennessee's state tree, concerns about height and storm vulnerability
* Eastern redbud: Aesthetic value, susceptibility to canker and leaf diseases
* Tree of Heaven: Invasive species concerns, aggressive root systems, rapid growth
* Red maple: Value for shade and fall color, concerns about surface roots
* Black walnut: Awareness of juglone toxicity affecting other plants, nut mess concerns
* Sweetgum: Frustration with spiky seed balls, root issues, but appreciation of shade
* Box elder: Often considered less desirable, concerns about box elder bugs
* American beech: Valued heritage trees, concerns about beech bark disease
* Disease awareness: Familiarity with anthracnose symptoms (leaf spots, twig dieback), oak wilt (rapid leaf drop), powdery mildew (white coating)
