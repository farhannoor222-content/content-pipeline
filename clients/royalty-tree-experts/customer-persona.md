# Royalty Tree Experts Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview

Royalty Tree Experts serves established property owners across Macomb, St. Clair, Sanilac, Lapeer, and Oakland counties, anchored around Fort Gratiot Township, MI. The typical client is a middle-aged or older homeowner or small commercial property manager who deeply values property safety, mature tree preservation, and clean aesthetics. Managing spacious suburban lots or semi-rural acreage, these clients prioritize reliable, locally owned, and fully insured professional services when severe Great Lakes weather threatens their properties.

High homeownership stability (over 81% homeownership rate), deeply rooted in the local community, fiercely protective of their property investments, and prone to handling home improvements through trusted local word-of-mouth recommendations.

## Decision-Making Process

### 1. Initial Trigger Events

* Severe seasonal storms, high winds, or heavy ice common to Eastern Michigan dropping large limbs onto roofs, fences, or driveways.
* Dead, diseased, or dying ash and oak trees posing an immediate hazard to structures or utility lines.
* Overgrown branches scraping against siding, blocking gutters, or choking out lawn sunlight.
* Unsightly leftover tree stumps from previous DIY cuts preventing proper lawn mowing or landscaping upgrades.

### 2. Research Phase

* Relying heavily on local Facebook community groups, neighborhood word-of-mouth recommendations, and Google search queries for local tree services.
* Checking credentials to confirm the business is fully licensed and insured to mitigate liability risks.
* Reviewing online star ratings and customer testimonials to verify honest communication and reliable scheduling.

### 3. Evaluation Criteria

* Clear proof of liability insurance and worker's compensation safety compliance.
* Transparent, upfront communication with zero hidden fees, supported by free, no-obligation estimates.
* Speed of response, particularly during regional weather emergencies when prompt cleanup is critical.
* Professional execution and thorough post-job clean-up (brush removal and rake-down).

### 4. Quote Process

* Expecting a prompt response via phone call, text, or email from an owner-operator or knowledgeable arborist.
* Appreciating an on-site or fast digital assessment that explains the precise scope of work—whether technical rigging is required near power lines or standard ground cutting.
* Valuing straightforward, fair pricing that reflects elite safety standards rather than the absolute cheapest bargain.

## Details about Specific Trees in the Area

### Common Species

* Eastern White Pine & Spruce: Towering evergreens prone to heavy snow accumulation and shallow root failures during high-wind saturation.
* Silver Maple & Red Oak: Massive canopy trees offering deep shade but frequently developing internal decay, weak branch unions, and deadwood.
* Ash Trees: Heavily impacted by regional pests like the Emerald Ash Borer, creating large volumes of brittle, dead standing timber requiring safe sectional removal.

## Weather Conditions for Trees

* Fluctuating Great Lakes weather patterns, ranging from humid summer thunderstorms packing high straight-line winds to severe winter ice storms that coat branches in heavy layers of freezing rain.

### Seasonal Challenges

* Winter & Early Spring: Heavy ice loads and wet snow snapping mature limbs and testing tree structural integrity.
* Late Summer: Severe convective storm cells dropping microbursts that uproot shallowly rooted trees in saturated soils.
