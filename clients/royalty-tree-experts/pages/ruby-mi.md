## How Wet Ground and Ice Shape Tree Work Here

Wet ground loosens a tree's grip, so our arborists check root zones first. That soil lets shallow-rooted white pine and spruce lean in wind, so we watch for tilted trunks. When a trunk tilts, our climbers take it down in sections so it cannot land on your house. Silver maple and red oak bring a different risk because their weak branch unions split under ice. Our tree surgeons climb in and cut those weak limbs so ice finds less to hold. Near the Black River and M-136, our experienced team rigs limbs down from roofs and wires. That slow lowering protects your roof and lines so storm cleanup stays safe and clean. As local tree care professionals, we plan each job around the soil and the trees on it.
