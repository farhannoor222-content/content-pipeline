## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

Old stumps block your mower, trip your family, and leave dead patches in your lawn. Our owner-operated team grinds each stump below grade with a stump grinder, so the roots break down instead of leaving a sunken spot. You get level ground that is ready for new sod or a replanted tree. We clean up the chips and sawdust before we leave. Royalty Tree Experts is licensed and insured, and our free, no-obligation estimate shows your cost before any work starts.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

When a storm drops a tree on your roof, driveway, or vehicle, you need help at any hour. Royalty Tree Experts answers emergency calls 24/7, and our owner-operated team cuts the tree into sections, uses rigging to lift limbs off your home, and clears the debris. That sectional cutting keeps heavy wood from falling on damage that is already there. We are licensed and insured, and our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee covers the work. Call us any time, and we will explain the plan before we start.
