## Common Tree Issues

## Fungal Diseases

1. Fire Blight
   * Affects crabapples, apples, and some pear trees.
   * Causes blackened, wilted leaves and branch dieback.
2. Cytospora Canker
   * Common in spruce and aspen.
   * Causes sunken, discolored bark and resin oozing from branches.
3. Needle Cast Disease
   * Found in spruces and pines.
   * Causes browning and loss of inner needles.
4. Anthracnose
   * Affects ash, maple, and sycamore trees.
   * Causes leaf spots, curling, and premature leaf drop.

5. Powdery Mildew
   * Affects many ornamentals, including lilacs and crabapples.
   * Leaves get a white, powdery coating.
6. Root Rot (various fungi)
   * Caused by poor drainage or overwatering.
   * Trees may show stunted growth and early leaf loss.

## Insect Pests

7. Mountain Pine Beetle
   * Attacks ponderosa and lodgepole pines.
   * Leaves behind boring dust and pitch tubes.
8. Emerald Ash Borer (EAB)
   * Highly destructive to ash trees (not confirmed widespread yet, but monitored).
   * Causes canopy thinning, bark splitting, and D-shaped exit holes.
9. Ips Beetles (Engraver Beetles)
   * Attack stressed pine trees.
   * Small holes and dry boring dust near the trunk base.
10. Aphids
   * Found on a variety of trees.
   * Cause sticky residue (honeydew) and curled leaves.
11. Spider Mites
   * Attack spruce and juniper.
   * Cause bronzed needles and fine webbing.
12. Scale Insects
   * Found on maple, aspen, and others.
   * Appear as small bumps; cause yellowing and dieback.

## Environmental & Physical Stress

13. Sunscald / Frost Cracks
   * Affects young trees with thin bark (e.g., maples).
   * Caused by sudden winter temperature drops.
14. Drought Stress
   * Common due to dry climate and poor watering habits.
   * Leads to wilting, leaf scorch, and dieback.
15. Wind Damage
   * Breaks limbs or uproots poorly anchored trees.
16. Compacted Soil & Root Damage
   * From construction or heavy foot traffic.
   * Restricts water and nutrient uptake.
