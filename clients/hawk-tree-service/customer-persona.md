* High winds causing large branches to fall across driveways and walkways
* Lightning strikes splitting tree trunks and creating immediate safety hazards

* Ice storms weighing down branches beyond their structural capacity limits
* Tornadoes uprooting entire trees and damaging surrounding property structures
* Heavy snow accumulation breaking limbs and threatening roof damage
* Trees falling onto homes, garages, or vehicles during severe weather
* Power lines entangled with damaged trees creating electrocution risks
* Emergency access blocked by fallen trees preventing first responder entry
* Immediate liability concerns when damaged trees threaten neighboring properties

* Yellowing or browning leaves outside normal seasonal color changes
* Dead branches accumulating throughout the tree canopy over time
* Visible pest infestations including bark beetles or aphid colonies
* Fungal growth appearing on trunk bark or around root systems
* Structural cracks developing in main trunk or major limb connections
* Lean development indicating potential root system failure or soil issues
* Sparse foliage growth compared to previous years or neighboring trees
* Early leaf drop occurring weeks before normal autumn timing
* Bark damage from lawn equipment or construction activity exposure
* Root exposure due to soil erosion or construction excavation nearby
