## What makes a tree "hazardous"? A homeowner's guide

A hazardous tree has a defect that could make it fail onto a home or car in **Colorado Springs, CO**. Many defects hide in plain sight.

Our arborists check every tree for a few common warning signs. Look for these red flags on your property:

✓ Dead or hanging limbs

✓ Deep trunk cracks

✓ Pine beetle holes

✓ Roots lifting soil

✓ Leaning toward structures
