# Hawk Tree Service

## GBP Product: Tree Service

**Product name:** Tree Service

**Description:**

Overgrown limbs, dead trees, and dry growth near your house raise your risk in every season, from heavy snow to wildfire. Our qualified arborists walk your property, mark the trees that need work, and explain each step before we start.

Hawk Tree Service then removes, prunes, and trims trees, grinds stumps, and clears fire-prone growth, using methods that protect your roof and landscaping. You get a free estimate and no hidden fees, so you know the full cost up front, plus a satisfaction guarantee and 24/7 emergency help. We are licensed and insured.
