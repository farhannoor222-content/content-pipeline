# Premier Tree Service Customer Profile

* Premier Tree Service - Customer Persona: The Established New Orleans Homeowner

## Target Client Overview

Middle to upper-middle-class homeowners in New Orleans who value their property's appearance and safety. These clients typically own homes in historic neighborhoods like the Garden District, Uptown, Lakeview, or other areas with mature tree coverage. They're often long-term residents who understand the unique challenges of maintaining properties in New Orleans' climate. Many are second or third-generation New Orleans residents who have inherited properties with established trees, while others are successful professionals who have purchased historic homes and want to maintain their character.

## Demographics

* Age: 35-65 years old
* Income: $75,000-$200,000 annually
* Education: 70% have Bachelor's degrees, 30% have advanced degrees
* Home Value: $350,000-$1,000,000
* Location: Primary focus on Orleans Parish, with service extending to Metairie, Kenner, and the North Shore
* Property Type:
   * Historic homes (40%)
   * Modern single-family homes (35%)
   * High-end townhouses (15%)
   * Small multi-family properties (10%)
* Average property size: 0.15-0.5 acres
* Average tree count per property: 3-8 mature trees

## Individual Overview

These homeowners are well-educated professionals who understand the value of professional tree care but lack technical knowledge about arboriculture. They're often busy with careers and family life, preferring to delegate property maintenance to trusted professionals. They're invested in their communities and understand that proper tree maintenance affects not just their property, but their neighborhood's overall appeal and safety.

### Key characteristics

* Highly educated but may lack specific knowledge about tree care
* Time-poor but financially stable
* Strong connection to New Orleans culture and history
* Active in community organizations
* Regular entertainers who value curb appeal
* Often work from home at least part-time
* Travel frequently for work or pleasure
* May have experienced previous property damage from storms

## Specific Trees in the Area

### Common Species

1. Live Oak (Quercus virginiana)
   * Age: 50-300+ years
   * Size: 40-80 ft height
   * Spread: 60-100 ft
   * Care needs: Regular pruning, root management
2. Southern Magnolia (Magnolia grandiflora)
   * Age: 30-80 years
   * Size: 60-80 ft height
   * Spread: 30-50 ft
   * Care needs: Leaf cleanup, pruning
3. Crepe Myrtle (Lagerstroemia indica)
   * Age: 15-50 years
   * Size: 15-25 ft height
   * Spread: 15-25 ft
   * Care needs: Annual pruning, shape maintenance
4. Bald Cypress (Taxodium distichum)
   * Age: 30-100+ years
   * Size: 50-70 ft height
   * Spread: 20-30 ft
   * Care needs: Knees management, thinning
5. Water Oak (Quercus nigra)
   * Age: 30-70 years
   * Size: 50-80 ft height
   * Spread: 40-60 ft
   * Care needs: Regular inspection, pruning
6. Pecan (Carya illinoinensis)
   * Age: 40-100+ years
   * Size: 60-100 ft height
   * Spread: 40-75 ft
   * Care needs: Regular pruning, disease management
7. Southern Pine (Pinus spp.)
   * Age: 30-80 years
   * Size: 60-100 ft height
   * Spread: 20-40 ft
   * Care needs: Disease monitoring, storm preparation
8. Sweet Gum (Liquidambar styraciflua)
   * Age: 30-60 years
   * Size: 60-75 ft height
   * Spread: 40-50 ft
   * Care needs: Ball cleanup, root management
