# Bonzo Excavating Customer Profile

## Overview of the Typical Client

### The typical Bonzo Excavating client falls into several distinct categories

### Residential Property Owners

* Middle to upper-middle class homeowners with properties in the Greater Pittsburgh area
* Typically own their homes for 5+ years and are making significant improvements
* Have saved specifically for property improvements or have secure financing
* Value working with local businesses with strong reputations
* Often planning long-term residence at their property (7+ years)
* Frequently have some connection to trades or construction through family or friends

### Small Commercial Property Owners

* Local business owners expanding their physical locations
* Property investors developing or improving small commercial spaces
* Often have previous experience with construction projects
* Seeking reliable contractors who understand commercial timelines and requirements
* Frequently combining several improvement projects simultaneously

### Contractors and Developers

* Professional builders needing excavation subcontractors
* Small to mid-sized development companies working on Pittsburgh-area projects
* Repeat customers who may provide multiple projects per year
* Highly knowledgeable about construction specifics
* Have established relationships with other trade professionals

## Decision-Making Process (Expanded)

1. Recognition of Need:
   * Client identifies a project requiring excavation (new construction, drainage issues, etc.)
   * Often triggered by property issues (standing water, erosion) or planned improvements
   * Frequently seasonal (spring planning for summer projects)
   * Sometimes emergency-based (sudden drainage failures, utility breaks)
   * May be part of a phased property improvement plan
2. Research Phase:
   * Searches online for local excavation contractors (Google, Yelp, HomeAdvisor)
   * Asks friends/contractors for recommendations (word-of-mouth very important)
   * Reviews social media for project photos and testimonials
   * Checks local business groups and Better Business Bureau ratings
   * Researches typical processes and pricing to establish baseline knowledge
   * For commercial projects, may issue formal RFPs to selected contractors
3. Evaluation:
   * Reviews company websites for professionalism and relevant experience
   * Checks Google reviews and testimonials (looking for consistent positive experiences)
   * Contacts 2-3 companies for initial conversations and quotes
   * Assesses responsiveness and communication quality during inquiry phase
   * Evaluates whether company understands their specific needs
   * For commercial clients, reviews provided portfolios and references
4. Site Assessment:
   * Arranges for in-person site visits with 2-3 potential contractors
   * Takes note of thoroughness in examination and questioning
   * Values contractors who identify potential issues proactively
   * Observes professionalism and knowledge demonstrated during visit
   * Receives detailed quotes with line-item breakdowns
   * May request phasing options or alternative approaches
5. Decision Factors:
   * Weighs quotes against perceived value and scope completeness
   * Evaluates contractor's ability to meet timeline requirements
   * Assesses communication clarity and thoroughness
   * Considers equipment appropriateness for the specific project
   * Reviews proposed solutions for demonstrated understanding
   * For repeated clients, previous experience weighs heavily
   * Values detailed contracts with clear specifications
6. Final Selection:
   * Chooses contractor based on trust, price, and ability to meet schedule
   * Often selects middle-range pricing if value proposition is strong
   * May negotiate scope adjustments to meet budget constraints
   * Reviews and signs detailed contract with specifications
   * Provides deposit (typically 25-30% of project cost)
   * Establishes communication protocols for project updates
7. Post-Service Evaluation:
   * Judges work quality and whether expectations were met
   * Evaluates site cleanliness and respect for property
   * Assesses whether timeline commitments were honored
   * Considers likelihood of recommending to others
   * For commercial clients, formal project closeout evaluation
   * May leave online reviews or provide testimonials
   * Determines potential for future projects with same contractor

## Service Model & Pricing (Expanded)

### Consultation Structure

* Initial Consultation: Free site assessment and project evaluation
   * Typical duration: 30-60 minutes on-site
   * Conducted by: Owner or senior foreman
   * Includes: Visual assessment, basic measurements, photo documentation
   * Deliverable: Initial verbal recommendations and approach options
   * Timeline: Typically available within 3-5 business days of request
   * Next steps: Detailed proposal development
* Secondary Consultation (if needed): Technical assessment for complex projects
   * Typical duration: 1-2 hours
   * Conducted by: Owner with specialized equipment if needed
   * Includes: Soil testing, elevation mapping, detailed measurements
   * Cost: Free for projects over $3,000, $150 fee (applied to project if selected) for smaller projects
   * Deliverable: Technical findings and specific approach recommendations
   * Timeline: Scheduled within 1 week of initial consultation

### Quote Structure

* Detailed line-item pricing for transparency
   * Separate line items for each major project component
   * Labor hours and rates clearly identified
   * Equipment costs separated from labor
   * Material quantities and unit prices listed
   * Allowances clearly marked for variable elements
   * Optional elements identified separately
* Proposal Documentation
   * Professional presentation with company branding
   * Project scope clearly defined in writing
   * Relevant photos of existing conditions included
   * Simple diagrams or sketches where helpful
   * Terms and conditions clearly stated
   * Process for changes and additions defined
   * Validity period (typically 30 days) specified

### Pricing Models

* Mix of hourly rates for equipment/operators and flat project fees
   * Equipment with operator: $150-$225/hour depending on size
   * Additional labor: $65-$85/hour per worker
   * Transportation/mobilization: Flat fee based on distance ($250-$500)
   * Specialty equipment rental: Passed through at cost + 15%
   * Materials: Cost + 20-25% for handling and warranty
   * Complete project packages: Fixed price based on detailed scope
* Discount Structures
   * Multi-project discount: 5-10% for bundled projects
   * Repeat customer discount: 5% for previous clients
   * Off-season discount (Nov-March): 5-10% when scheduling allows
   * Referral credits: $100-$250 for successful referrals
   * Early payment discount: 2% for full payment at project start

### Payment Schedule

* Typical payment structure for standard projects
   * 30% deposit upon contract signing
   * 30% progress payment at project midpoint
   * 30% upon substantial completion
   * 10% final payment after walk-through and approval
* Adjusted schedules for project types
   * Small projects under $2,000: 50% deposit, 50% upon completion
   * Large projects over $15,000: Custom schedule with multiple milestones
   * Extended duration projects: Monthly progress payments
   * Commercial projects: Net-30 terms available with approved credit
   * Public projects: Compliance with municipal payment requirements
