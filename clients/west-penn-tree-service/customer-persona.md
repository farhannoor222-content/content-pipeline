# West Penn Tree Service Customer Profile

## West Penn Tree Service - Comprehensive Business Profile

## Target Client Overview

West Penn Tree Service primarily serves homeowners aged 35-65 with middle to upper-middle income ($75,000-$150,000) who have completed some college education or higher. These clients typically own homes valued between $250,000-$600,000 in suburban and semi-rural areas around Pittsburgh, Saltsburg, Greensburg, Bethel Park, Latrobe, and Penn Hills. The property portfolio consists of approximately 65% modern single-family homes, 20% historic homes, 10% high-end townhouses, and 5% small multi-family properties.
