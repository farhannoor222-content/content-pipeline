# West Penn Tree Service

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

When a storm drops a tree across your roof, driveway, or fence, the danger does not wait for business hours. Our team is open 24 hours, 7 days, and we start by sizing up the lean, the weight, and the damage underneath.

West Penn Tree Service cuts the fallen tree into sections and lifts each one clear, so heavy wood stays off your home. We haul away the limbs and brush, and we are fully insured. You get a free, detailed estimate with no hidden costs, safe access to your property, and a clean yard when the work ends.
