# West Penn Tree Service

## GBP Service: Emergency Tree Service

**Service name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

A storm can drop a tree across your roof or driveway at any hour. West Penn Tree Service is open 24 hours, 7 days, and cuts the fallen tree into sections so heavy wood stays off the damage. You get safe access to your home and a cleaned-up property. We are fully insured, and our estimates are free.
