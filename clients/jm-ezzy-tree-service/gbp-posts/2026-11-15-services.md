**Description:**

A tree too close to your house or power lines has to come down in sections. JM Ezzy Tree Service lowers each piece with ropes and rigging, then hauls the wood away. You get a clear yard and a protected roof. Ask for a free estimate.

## GBP Service: Tree Stump Removal

**Service name:** Tree Stump Removal

**Description:**

Stumps block new landscaping and trip your family. Our team digs around the base, cuts the roots loose, and lifts the stump out. That gives you open ground to replant or build on. JM Ezzy Tree Service is licensed and insured. Request a free estimate.

## GBP Service: Tree Trimming & Pruning

**Service name:** Tree Trimming & Pruning

**Description:**

Trimming and pruning keep limbs off your roof and power lines. Our team cuts each limb back to a healthy branch, so the tree heals cleanly and keeps its shape. We haul away the cuttings. Call JM Ezzy Tree Service for a free estimate.

## GBP Service: Tree Planting

**Service name:** Tree Planting

**Description:**

New trees add shade and curb appeal. JM Ezzy Tree Service digs the hole, sets each tree at the right depth, and backfills with soil so the roots settle. You get a healthy start for years of growth. Request a free estimate.
