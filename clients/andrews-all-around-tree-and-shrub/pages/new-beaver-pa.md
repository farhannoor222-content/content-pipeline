## River Flats and Hillsides Shape Our Work

Soft ground near the river loosens roots after rain, so tall trees can lean. Wet months raise that risk, since saturated soil gives a heavy tree little to hold. Our arborists use a different method on the Beaver River flats and the hills east of PA 168. On the flats they check each leaning trunk for heaved soil. Then they cut back heavy limbs so the tree is less likely to fall on your home. Up the hills, sloped ground limits where equipment can reach. Our climbers cut from the tree and lower each piece on ropes. Your roof and fence stay clear. Snow and ice also weigh down maple, oak and white pine limbs. Our team clears dead wood each autumn so those limbs hold the load.
