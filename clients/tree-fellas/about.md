### About Tree Fellas

At Tree Fellas, we serve Concord, NH, and the neighboring areas such as Bow, Hopkinton, and Loudon with pride. We are aware of the unique challenges trees in this part of the country face and what makes tree owners suffer. With experienced arborists, we help treat, maintain, and handle these trees and situations.

We combine art and science and follow modern tree care techniques. Each project we undertake goes through professional inspections. Our primary concern remains your safety.

From stump removal to complete tree care, we do it all. Our equipment helps us tackle challenging projects with confidence. We boast of ensuring the best care in the area. Each member of our squad works with completed dedication and commitment to providing industry-standard service.
