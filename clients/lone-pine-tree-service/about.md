## About Company

Lone Pine Tree Service provides tree service in the Amador, Calaveras, and San Joaquin Counties. If you are looking for a quality tree service, consider Lone Pine Tree Service. We provide a variety of services such as tree trimming/pruning, tree removal, stump grinding, arborist services, land clearing, and emergency tree services. We are fully licensed and insured. Our goal is to provide you with a great experience from start to finish. Lone Pine Tree Service is the company for you. We are experienced, professional, and affordable, and we will take care of your trees like they are our own. Contact us today or learn more about our services or book a free consultation.
