## Our Services

Older Hillcrest yards tend to face the same few problems: storm-weakened limbs, crowded canopies, and leftover stumps. Our tree care professionals match each service to the problem it solves.

### Tree Removal

A hollow laurel oak or decaying water oak can split and hit your roof in a storm. Our crew takes these trees down section by section and lowers each piece away from your house.

### Tree Trimming

Overgrown limbs on live oaks, laurel oaks, and magnolias can scrape shingles and shade out your lawn. We cut them back at the branch collar so each tree heals cleanly and stays balanced.

### Tree Cutting

Taking a tree down piece by piece adds time when a yard has clear open space. In those yards, our tree surgeons fell the tree whole along a planned line.

### Stump Removal

Old stumps can draw termites and send up sprouts across your lawn. The crew grinds each stump below grade so you can lay new sod or plant in that spot.

### Emergency Tree Service

Summer thunderstorms and tropical systems can drop limbs on roofs, cars, and fences without warning. Our team answers 24/7 and clears fallen trees from your home, driveway, or yard once it's safe.
