# Tree Service Near Zelienople Community Park: Shade and Play Along East Beaver Street

Zelienople Community Park covers more than 35 acres along East Beaver Street, next to Connoquenessing Valley Elementary School. A nonprofit organized it in 1949, and the borough took it over in 2003.

Families fill it for ballfields, lighted tennis courts, picnic shelters, a skate park and the Rotary Dog Park. A walking trail also draws neighbors from Zelienople and nearby Harmony.

Maples, oaks and sycamores shade yards around the park, and the Connoquenessing Creek valley keeps the soil damp. Damp soil loosens roots over time.

Summer storms and winter snow then load limbs that hang over fields, cars and rooftops. Regular trimming and early removal of weak trees keep visitors and neighbors safe.
