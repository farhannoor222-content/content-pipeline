# Cliburn Woodwork Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview (60 words)

Our ideal client is a homeowner between 30 and 65 who values safety, reliability, and clear communication. They care about protecting their property and want work done correctly the first time. They prefer local companies with strong reviews and consistent service quality. Budget matters, but trust and professionalism matter more. They expect honest estimates, dependable scheduling, and respectful crews. Most are busy families or working professionals who want straightforward service without stress or surprises.
