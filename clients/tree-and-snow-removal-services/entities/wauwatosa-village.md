# Tree Service Near Wauwatosa Village: Caring for Tosa's Historic Center

Wauwatosa Village is the historic center of Wauwatosa, Wisconsin, just west of Milwaukee. It grew along Plank Road, Harwood Avenue and State Street. The area began as Hart's Mills and took its Potawatomi name, meaning firefly, in the 1840s.

Its Little Red Store dates to 1854. Today brick buildings along the walkable district house independent shops, cafes and restaurants.

Trees shade the terraces and side streets here. In some blocks, ash trees line street after street.

Emerald ash borer, road salt and cramped terrace strips stress them, so early inspections matter. Timely trimming and removal protect walkways, storefronts and roofs.
