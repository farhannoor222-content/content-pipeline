### Common Tree Problems

* Fungal Diseases:
* Anthracnose:

A common fungal disease affecting the foliage of deciduous trees like maples, oaks, and ash. It can cause irregular brown leaf spots and twig dieback.

* Oak Wilt:

A devastating disease caused by a fungus that affects oak trees, leading to rapid leaf wilting, browning, and premature leaf drop.

* Dutch Elm Disease:

A fungal disease spread by elm bark beetles, causing leaves to curl and turn gray-green or yellow, eventually leading to death.

* Verticillium Wilt:

A typically fatal disease affecting a wide range of deciduous trees, especially maples and ash.

* Needlecast:

A disease that affects pines and spruces, causing needles to turn yellow and brown.

* Armillaria Root Rot:

A fungal infection that can affect many tree species, often weakening the tree and making it susceptible to other problems.

* Powdery Mildew:

A fungal disease that appears as white, powdery patches on leaves and stems.

* Insect Infestations:
   * Emerald Ash Borer: An invasive beetle that has been devastating ash trees in Wisconsin.
   * Gypsy Moth: A defoliating insect that can cause significant damage to trees, especially oaks.
   * Japanese Beetles: Can cause extensive defoliation on various trees and shrubs.
* Other Problems:
   * Fire Blight: A bacterial disease that affects the leaves and twigs of fruit trees.
   * Iron Chlorosis: A nutrient deficiency that can cause yellowing of leaves.
