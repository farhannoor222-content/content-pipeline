## About Georgia Weather

## Georgia Soil Type

The Georgia soils are nearly level to very steep soils on glaciated uplands. They are on broad plains and on the tops and side slopes of hills, ridges, and knolls. The soils formed in loamy till. The till is derived mainly from weathered limestone, shale, or slate with small amounts of granite.
