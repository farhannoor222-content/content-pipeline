## Common Trees in the Region

* Oaks: White, black, northern red, scarlet, and shingle oaks are prevalent.
* Maples: The Red Maple is highly common throughout the state.
* Flowering/Ornamental: Eastern redbud, which blooms pink in early April.
* Others: Poplars, locusts, and lindens are often found in urban settings.

## Common Tree Diseases and Pests in Western PA

* Beech Leaf Disease: Prevalent throughout Pennsylvania, this nematode-driven disease affects beech trees, causing wrinkled leaves and potentially killing trees within 2–10 years.
* Emerald Ash Borer (EAB): This invasive beetle is causing massive mortality in ash trees across the state.
* Fungal & Bacterial Diseases: Local trees frequently suffer from oak wilt, anthracnose, verticillium wilt, needlecast, fire blight, and bacterial leaf scorch.
* Other Pests: Hemlock trees are threatened by the woolly adelgid.

## Environmental and Structural Threats

* Weather & Soil Stress: Extreme weather, heatwaves, and drought put significant stress on local trees, weakening them.
* Wind and Rain: Strong winds paired with heavy rainfall are causing increased tree failures and uprooting.
* Construction Damage: Physical damage to tree roots and trunks during construction causes long-term decline.

## Signs of Tree Distress

* Curling, wilting, or discolored leaves.
* Early or unusual leaf drop.
* Thinning or uneven canopy.
* Mold, fungus, or soft/peeling bark.
* Lumps or knots on branches.
