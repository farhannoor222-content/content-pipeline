# Leatherneck Tree Service

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

A storm can drop a tree across your driveway, roof, or car in minutes, and more damage often hides under the wood. Our veteran-owned team sizes up the danger first, then makes the scene safe before any cutting starts.

Leatherneck Tree Service cuts the fallen tree into sections and lifts each piece clear with ropes and rigging, so heavy wood stays off the damage. We haul away the limbs and brush, and our 24-hour storm cleanup means you can call any time. You get open access to your property, a free estimate, and a clear plan for repairs.
