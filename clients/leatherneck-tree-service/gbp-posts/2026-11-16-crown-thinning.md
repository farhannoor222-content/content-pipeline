# Leatherneck Tree Service

## GBP Service: Crown Thinning

**Service name:** Crown Thinning

**Description:**

A dense crown catches wind and blocks light from your lawn. Leatherneck Tree Service thins it by cutting selected inner branches back to the trunk or a larger limb, so air and sunlight pass through. You get a lighter, storm-ready tree that keeps its shape. Ask us for a free estimate.
