## Snapshot

Fants Tree Service is a third-generation, family-owned tree company in business since 1978. It is based at 244 Crooked Run Ln, Culpeper, VA, and works across Albemarle County, Lake Monticello, Palmyra, Madison, Gordonsville and nearby towns. It runs three full crews of its own employees on its own equipment, including cranes and lifts. It does no subcontracting and no equipment rental. Besides tree work, it plows snow (many HOAs) and does mulch and hardscaping.

## Owner's Story

Matt's grandfather started the business in 1978 because he cared about trees and taking care of trees in Albemarle. Matt grew up around the work and likes trees. He took it over to keep the family business going. The family says it started in the 70s but was not formally licensed until "maybe 2000 something". ## Local Knowledge

* White oaks and chestnut oaks are common. An oak borer (a beetle, per Matt) is killing many of them right now.
* Pine and tulip poplar are among the weakest trees. They blow over in thunderstorms.
* Most areas they serve do not need a permit or HOA approval to remove a tree.
* Exception: the huge Main Street tree in Madison County needed a Board of Supervisors vote and VDOT approval to close the road.
* Busiest months are June, July and August, with thunderstorms.
* Best time to prune: spring and fall.
* Best time to remove: summer and all winter.
* Over-opening an oak can sun-scorch it.

## What Affects Price

* Size of the tree.
* Location: front or back yard.
* How close it is to the house. It may need a rig or a crane.
* How long the job will take.
* Danger matters less because they have equipment to work around it.
* Emergency work has no set fee. Each situation is priced case by case, based on severity.
* Snow plowing is priced per driveway.

## What Homeowners Get Wrong

* People think you can hack at a tree or cut the top out of it. That is not proper pruning.
* Good pruning is a weight reduction prune or a center prune.
* Don't open up an oak too much, or the sun will scorch it.
* Not every tree is diseased or needs to come down. Matt says they won't push work that isn't needed.
