## Our Working Process

Fant's Tree Service follows a safety-focused process on every stump grinding job. Each step relies on the right gear and trained hands.

### On-Site Assessment

We measure each stump and check for rocks and buried lines first. That check tells us how deep the grinder can go.

### Lay Arbor Mats

Our crew sets arbor mats along the path the grinder will travel. The mats carry the machine's weight over soft or wet ground.

The grinder's cutting wheel chews the stump below the soil line. We work in passes until the wood sits low enough for new grass.
