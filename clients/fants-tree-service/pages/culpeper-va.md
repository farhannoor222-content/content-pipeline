## Our Services

Our three crews bring their own cranes, lifts, and rigging to your Culpeper job. That equipment covers the full range of tree work below, from pruning to storm cleanup.

### Tree Removal

We take trees down section by section and rope each piece to the ground. Our cranes and lifts reach the high limbs on white oaks, tulip poplars, and pines near your home. Arbor mats under the equipment keep your lawn from rutting while the work moves along.

### Stump Grinding

Our crew grinds stumps below the soil line so you can mow, plant, or build over the spot. The grinder handles dense oak stumps in your yard as easily as softer pine and poplar. Roots left underground break down on their own over the next few years.

### Tree Trimming and Pruning

Rather than hacking a tree back to stubs, we use a weight reduction prune or a center prune. That approach lightens heavy white oak, chestnut oak, and tulip poplar limbs without opening the canopy too far. Oaks opened up too much can sun-scorch, so we leave enough cover to shade the bark.

### Crane Service

Our crane folds up for tight driveways and extends out to reach trees behind your house. It lifts large sections over roofs and fences instead of dropping them in the yard. We own the crane outright, so you never pay a rental markup for it.

### Tree Cutting

With open ground around a tree, we fell it in one piece using a notch and back cut. Each trunk lands in a clear zone that our crew picks away from your house and fences. Felling whole pines and tulip poplars in open fields saves time and keeps your cost down.

### Stump Removal

Planning a patio, walkway, or new bed calls for stump removal rather than grinding. We dig out the stump and its main roots so the ground is ready to build on. Since we also install patios and hardscaping, one crew can carry the project from stump to stone.

### Emergency Tree Service

Day or night, we answer storm calls and price each one case by case. Our crews clear the pines and tulip poplars that summer thunderstorms throw across roofs and driveways. Owning our cranes and lifts means we start without waiting on rental equipment.

### Cabling and Bracing

Steel cables and braces from our crew support split trunks and weak forks on valuable trees. This hardware can keep your large white oak or chestnut oak standing instead of coming down. We check each tree first and tell you honestly whether cabling will help.

### Tree Health and Plant Care

Feeding stressed trees and checking your oaks for borer damage are part of our plant care visits. A borer beetle is killing many white and chestnut oaks around Culpeper right now. Our crew tells you whether a tree can recover and never invents a disease to sell a removal.
