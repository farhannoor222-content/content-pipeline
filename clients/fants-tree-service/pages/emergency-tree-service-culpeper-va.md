## Our Working Process

Fant's Tree Service follows a safety-focused process on every emergency call. Even at night, each step happens in the same order.

### On-Site Assessment

We check for downed lines, cracked limbs, and roof damage first. If a line is involved, we call the utility before cutting.

### Lay Arbor Mats

Our crew lays arbor mats over soaked ground before moving in heavy equipment. The mats stop machines from sinking into storm-softened soil.

### Rig Out and Rope Down

We rig your damaged tree and lower each piece by rope or crane. That control keeps pieces from rolling onto your roof.

### Cleanup and Walkthrough

Our crew clears debris from your driveway and yard. Then we walk the property with you and point out any remaining risks.
