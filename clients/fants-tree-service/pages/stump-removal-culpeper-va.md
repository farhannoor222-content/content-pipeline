## Our Working Process

Fant's Tree Service follows a safety-focused process on every stump removal job. Each step protects your lawn and your buried lines.

### Locate Lines and Roots

Public utilities get marked through Virginia 811, but irrigation and pet-fence wires do not. Our crew checks for those before any digging.

### Protect the Turf Path

Heavy machines rut soft ground fast. Our crew lays arbor mats to spread the load, so your lawn survives the trip in and out.

### Lift Out the Root Ball

Digging around the stump first limits how much soil leaves with it. Our machines then pull the stump out with its main roots.

### Fill, Level, and Review

A leftover hole is a trip hazard for kids and mowers. We fill and level the spot, then walk it with you before leaving.

Yes, call 811 about 48 hours before digging starts, and the call is free. Our crew confirms the marks are in place before we start.
