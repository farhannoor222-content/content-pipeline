## When a Tree Can Fall in One Piece

We only fell a tree whole when its landing zone is longer than the tree is tall. Our crew measures that space before choosing between cutting and section-by-section removal. If you own open acreage around Culpeper, your pines and tulip poplars likely have the room we need. For trees beside Route 29 or other state roads, we check whether VDOT approval is needed. Most private properties we serve need no permit or HOA approval for felling. We also look for your fences, sheds, and power lines that could rule out a full fell. If the space falls short, we switch to rigging and our crane so your property stays safe.

## Our Working Process

Fant's Tree Service runs every tree cutting job with a safety-focused plan. You can follow each step from a safe distance.

### On-Site Assessment

You watch us pace off the landing zone and check for fences and lines. We show you where the trunk will land.

### Lay Arbor Mats

Our crew lays mats wherever equipment will cross your lawn. Those mats keep the ground firm and free of ruts.

### Notch and Fell

Next, our sawyer cuts a notch on the landing side and then a back cut. You watch the trunk tip into the cleared zone.

### Cleanup and Walkthrough

The crew then picks up the mats and clears the area. You walk the site with us before we leave.
