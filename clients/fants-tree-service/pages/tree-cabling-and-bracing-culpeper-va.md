## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does tree cabling cost?

Cost guides list cabling at about $250 to $1,500 per tree, and bracing rods add to that. Our free estimates set your exact price.

### Can cabling save an oak damaged by borers?

No, cables hold a weak fork together but cannot revive an oak that borers are killing. Our arborists tell you which trees are worth supporting.

### Will cabling keep my tree up in a storm?

Cabling limits fork movement in wind but cannot hold a tree whose roots lift. Our arborists check the whole tree before any cable goes up.
