## Our Working Process

Fant's Tree Service follows a safety-focused process on every crane job. Each step matches the machine and the rigging to the tree in front of us.

### Size Up the Lift

Our tree experts estimate trunk weight and the reach the crane needs. They check the ground and overhead lines before the machine moves in.

### Set the Crane Base

The crane folds up for the drive in and extends out once parked. Arbor mats go down first to carry its weight.

### Rig and Cut Sections

Our arborists rig each section and make the cut. The crane takes the weight and swings the piece to a safe landing spot.

### Fold Down and Review

The crew folds the crane back down for the road. Cut sections leave the work area, and you walk the site before we go.
