## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does tree health care cost?

National cost guides put feeding a tree at about $100 to $350, and mulching or pruning adds to that. Our free estimates set your price.

### What signs show a tree needs help?

Thin foliage, dead branches or early leaf drop signal stress. Our arborists examine the tree and tell you whether it needs help.

### Can mulch hurt my tree?

Yes, mulch piled against the trunk can cause decay, and Virginia foresters warn against it. Our team keeps mulch off the trunk.

### Does a struggling tree need removal?

Not always, since many stressed trees recover with feeding and mulching. Our arborists tell you the truth when a tree is past saving.
