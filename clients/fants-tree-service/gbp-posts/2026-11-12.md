## How Culpeper, VA Homeowners Can Prepare Their Trees for the First Snow

The first snow can arrive wet and heavy, so weak limbs crack early. A fallen branch can damage your roof or driveway.
Pruning before the storm arrives fixes that. In **Culpeper, VA**, our climbers cut dead wood from pines and use a weight reduction prune on tulip poplars. We rope each limb down, so nothing lands on your home.
