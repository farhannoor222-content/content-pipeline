# Perkins Lawn Care Customer Profile

## Target Client Overview (60 words)

Homeowners in Kalamazoo, MI, and surrounding areas rely on professional tree care for safety, aesthetics, and property maintenance. They seek expertise, affordability, and trustworthiness. Many own single-family homes with mature trees that require regular trimming, removal, or disease management. Emergency services are crucial after storms, and discounts attract seniors, veterans, and first responders. These customers value eco-friendly practices, compliance with city regulations, and expert advice on tree health and maintenance.

## Demographics

* Age: 35-65 years old
* Income: $60,000 - $150,000 annually
* Education: High school diploma, college degree, or higher
* Home Value: $200,000 - $500,000
* Location: Kalamazoo, MI, and nearby neighborhoods
* Property Type:
   * Historic homes: 15%
   * Modern single-family homes: 60%
   * High-end townhouses: 15%
   * Small multi-family properties: 10%

## Key Characteristics of the Customer

* Homeowners who prioritize property aesthetics, safety, and longevity
* Long-term residents who invest in regular tree maintenance
* Individuals seeking professional, insured tree services
* Environmentally conscious customers concerned about tree health and sustainability
* Budget-conscious but willing to pay for high-quality, reliable service

## Decision-Making Process

1. Initial Trigger Events:
   * Storm damage or fallen trees
   * Overgrown branches threatening power lines or property
   * Tree diseases affecting lawn health
   * Home improvements requiring landscaping adjustments
2. Research Phase:
   * Google searches for "tree removal Kalamazoo" or "tree trimming services near me"
   * Checking online reviews and testimonials
   * Asking neighbors or friends for recommendations
3. Evaluation Criteria:
   * Company reputation and ratings
   * Experience and expertise
   * Price transparency and available discounts
   * Licensing, insurance, and safety compliance
4. Quote Process:
   * Requesting a free estimate
   * Comparing prices with competitors
   * Confirming availability and service timeline

## Common Trees in Kalamazoo, MI

* Red Maple: Vibrant fall color, prone to ice damage
* Sugar Maple: Sturdy and long-lived, susceptible to leaf scorch
* White Oak: Strong, slow-growing, supports local wildlife
* Black Walnut: Valuable wood but toxic to certain plants
* Honey Locust: Hardy with small leaves, minimal cleanup
* Box Elder: Fast-growing but prone to storm damage
* Eastern Hemlock: Provides dense shade, sensitive to pests
* Tulip Tree: Tall and fast-growing, requires regular pruning

## Seasonal Tree Challenges

* Spring: Risk of fungal infections, ideal pruning season
* Summer: Storms cause fallen limbs and structural damage
* Fall: Heavy leaf drop, preparation for winter storms
* Winter: Snow and ice accumulation lead to branch breakage
