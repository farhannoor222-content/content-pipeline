## Common Trees in Kalamazoo, MI

* Red Maple: Vibrant fall color, prone to ice damage
* Sugar Maple: Sturdy and long-lived, susceptible to leaf scorch
* White Oak: Strong, slow-growing, supports local wildlife
* Black Walnut: Valuable wood but toxic to certain plants
* Honey Locust: Hardy with small leaves, minimal cleanup
* Box Elder: Fast-growing but prone to storm damage
* Eastern Hemlock: Provides dense shade, sensitive to pests
* Tulip Tree: Tall and fast-growing, requires regular pruning

## Seasonal Tree Challenges

* Spring: Risk of fungal infections, ideal pruning season
* Summer: Storms cause fallen limbs and structural damage
* Fall: Heavy leaf drop, preparation for winter storms
* Winter: Snow and ice accumulation lead to branch breakage

## Problems the Service Solves

* Emergency Issues: Fallen trees blocking roads, storm-damaged limbs
* Maintenance Challenges: Overgrown branches, seasonal pruning needs
* Safety Concerns: Diseased trees, root damage to sidewalks
* Regulatory Compliance: HOA and city tree regulations
