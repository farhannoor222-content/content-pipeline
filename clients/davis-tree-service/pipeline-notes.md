## Client-Specific Notes
- Ponca City code: property owners must keep vegetation from obstructing streets, sidewalks, trails, alleys, and public ways, and must remove dead, decayed, or broken branches that overhang public travel areas. Useful for regulatory angles and FAQ permit answers.
- Trees on or near power lines: customers should contact their electric utility. Never suggest Davis Tree Service cuts near energized lines without utility coordination.
- Construction-related tree work may need permit guidance from Ponca City Building Services. Never promise that permits are never needed.
- Never promise a universal emergency arrival time. Describe emergency priority honestly.
- Say "older homes" or "pre-1939 housing," not "historic homes," unless a property is in a verified historic district. No claims of historic-preservation expertise.
- Say "licensed arborist," not "ISA-certified" (the source files only say licensed).
- No claims of being the cheapest or best-reviewed company. Lead with scope clarity, safety, cleanup, and equipment capability.
- No environmental or reuse claims unless the client confirms the practice.
- Use the 100% Satisfaction Guarantee only as worded in about.md. Do not expand it into other guarantees.
- Audience: plain-language explanations, property-protection framing over luxury framing (customer-persona.md).
