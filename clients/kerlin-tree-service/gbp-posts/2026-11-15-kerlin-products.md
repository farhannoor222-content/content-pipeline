# Kerlin Tree Service

## GBP Product: Stump Removal

**Product name:** Stump Removal

**Description:**

Stump removal takes the entire stump and its main roots out of your yard. Our team digs around the base, cuts the roots free, and lifts the stump out of the ground. That leaves open space you can level, replant, or build on. Our site cleanup is included, so the stump and debris leave with us. Kerlin Tree Service is licensed and insured, which protects your property during the dig. Request a free, no-obligation estimate, and we will explain the method and cost before work begins.

## GBP Product: Stump Grinding

**Product name:** Stump Grinding

**Description:**

A stump grinder cuts your stump into wood chips, so nothing is left to trip on or mow around. Our operator sweeps the grinder's cutting wheel across the stump and takes it down to the depth you agree on. That depth leaves room for new grass or a replanted tree. We clean up the site afterward, so you get a level yard without the stump. Kerlin Tree Service is licensed and insured. Ask for a free, no-obligation estimate to see your cost up front.

## GBP Product: Emergency Tree Service

**Product name:** Emergency Tree Service

**Description:**

A fallen or leaning tree can block your driveway or threaten your home at any hour. Kerlin Tree Service answers emergency calls 24/7, and our team arrives with a bucket truck, chainsaws, and a wood chipper. We cut the tree into sections, clear your access, and haul the debris away. When a tree is too large or unstable to climb, we bring in a crane. We explain emergency rates before work begins, and we are licensed and insured.
