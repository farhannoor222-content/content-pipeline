# 2026-11-27 — Atomic Habits (James Clear) — Fact hook, ~35s

**Problem:** Amra shobai ek lafe boro change chai. Notun bochor, notun plan, boro boro goal. Kintu 2-3 diner moddhei shob thame jay. Keno?
