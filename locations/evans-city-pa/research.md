# Location Research: Evans City, PA

Researched 2026-11-04 for Keystone Tree Techs. Facts come from web search results; source pages could not be opened from the research session, so confirm anything critical before reuse.

## Population and Tier

- 1,737 (2020 Census, per Wikipedia; other sites give 1,740 to 1,741)
- Tier 1 (under 5,000)
- Borough in southwestern Butler County, about 28 miles north of Pittsburgh, in the valley of Breakneck Creek. Settled 1830, incorporated 1882.

## Nickname and Local Terms

- No official nickname. Known as the filming location of Night of the Living Dead (1968) and The Crazies (1973).
- PA 68 runs through town as Main Street. I-79 Exit 83 nearby.

## Tree Regulations

- No borough tree ordinance found in search results. Not confirmed either way.
- Spotted lanternfly: Butler County joined Pennsylvania's quarantine in March 2023, after sightings in Jackson and Cranberry townships.

## Local Tree Problems and Climate

- Breakneck Creek and Connoquenessing Creek flooding: Evans City is among the communities hit hardest when they overflow. A regional study recommended stormwater improvements in the northeast part of town rather than creek flood control. Evans City, Jackson Township, Zelienople, and Harmony are working on a joint stormwater authority.
- Spotted lanternfly (county quarantine since 2023). Feeds on maples, black walnut, and tree of heaven.
- Region-wide from customer-persona.md: anthracnose, oak wilt, winter storm damage. (Persona also lists fire blight and sudden oak death; sudden oak death is not established in Pennsylvania, so don't use it.)
- Soil: creek valley floodplain soil (no borough-specific soil survey found)

## Neighborhoods and Landmarks

- Main Street (PA 68): central business strip. Living Dead Museum is on Main Street (per a 2018 article; current status unconfirmed).
- Evans City Cemetery: chapel restored in 2014, appears in Night of the Living Dead. Historical marker at 204 South Jackson Street.
- EDCO Park: named for Edward Dambach, who left the former Dambach lumber mill site to the borough. Pool, pavilions, courts. Address listed as 154 W. Main St. or 204-B South Jackson St. (conflict).
- Breakneck Creek: flows northwest through the valley toward Eidenau, where it joins Connoquenessing Creek.
- South Washington Street and Center Alley: second Night of the Living Dead marker.
