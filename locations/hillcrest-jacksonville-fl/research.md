# Location Research: Hillcrest, Jacksonville, FL

Researched 2026-11-04 for Show Me Tree Service (neighborhood page). Facts come from web search results; source pages could not be opened from the research session, so confirm anything critical before reuse.

## Population and Tier

- About 7,045 (Statistical Atlas neighborhood figure)
- Tier 2 (5,000 to 20,000)
- Westside/Southwest Jacksonville, ZIP codes 32205 and 32210, about 7 miles southwest of Downtown. NeighborhoodScout classes it as suburban, lower-middle income. Homes.com average value about $218,000.

## Streets and Landmarks

- Astral Street (5135 built 1947), Hollycrest Drive (5105 built 1948), Lexington Avenue (5327 built 1950), Kingsbury Street: rental listings. Late-1940s and early-1950s housing.
- Lenox Avenue and Normandy Boulevard addresses placed in Hillcrest by Walk Score. Normandy Boulevard has many of the area's shops.
- Nearby parks (Walk Score): John D. Liverman Park, Arques Park, Criswell Park.
- Near Riverside High School; easy access to I-10 and Cassat Avenue.
- Boundary caveat: some Cassat and Normandy addresses are labeled Murray Hill or Normandy by other sources.

## Trees, Soil, Climate

- Laurel oak and water oak: fast-growing, weaker wood, shorter lives; laurel oak is among the most common large trees to fall in Florida hurricanes (UF/IFAS-derived ranking via News4Jax).
- Live oak and sand live oak: dense wood, more wind resistant.
- Sandy, fast-draining coastal soil; shallow-rooted trees tip more easily.
- Laurel wilt has hit redbay, camphor, and avocado in Jacksonville (tree-service blog, weaker source).
- Hurricane season starts June 1; summer thunderstorms and tropical systems.

## Tree Regulations

- Jacksonville Zoning Code Chapter 656 Part 12 protects certain trees, but single-family lots are reported exempt (sources conflict on details).
- Florida Statute 163.045 limits local permits for residential tree removal when an ISA-certified arborist or licensed landscape architect documents a danger. Show Me Tree Service's arborist is not listed as certified, so don't promise permit exemptions. Not used on the page.
