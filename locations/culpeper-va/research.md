## Tree Regulations

- No Town of Culpeper tree removal ordinance found in search. Culpeper County has a zoning-based Landscape Ordinance, which does not address private tree removal permits.
- Client says most areas they serve need no permit or HOA approval. Not confirmed for the town specifically; check the Town Code before writing permit content.

## Local Tree Problems

- From the client: an oak borer (beetle) killing many white and chestnut oaks; pines and tulip poplars blowing over in June to August thunderstorms.
- Two-lined chestnut borer is a known contributor to oak decline in Virginia (likely the beetle the owner means, not confirmed).
- Spotted lanternfly: local news reports lanternflies returning to Culpeper and Rappahannock counties (date unclear). Virginia's quarantine was repealed effective March 27, 2025, and Culpeper was never on the quarantine list. Not used on the page.
- Emerald ash borer: statewide threat, no Culpeper-specific detection confirmed. Not used.

## Neighborhoods and Landmarks

- Davis Street / historic downtown: shops, restaurants, brick crosswalks, historic storefronts.
- Museum of Culpeper History: 113 S. Commerce St., in the historic train depot.
- Yowell Meadow Park: Blue Ridge Ave and Route 522 North; 1-mile paved trail, nature trail along Mountain Run, skate park, softball field, basketball courts.
- Mountain Run Lake Park: walking trails.
- Culpeper National Cemetery: 305 U.S. Ave.
- Rockwater Park: 32 acres of rolling farmland, trails, creek access, birdwatching.
- Lake Pelham: fishing reservoir (channel catfish, largemouth bass, crappie, bluegill).
- Lake Culpeper: 18019 Country Store Dr., kayaking and paddleboarding.
